Getting started in the server

The server is English-first and organized around self-selected roles. After joining, open the role menu and pick the games you actually play plus your region, so LFG posts and ping notifications match your schedule. Division 2 channels cover co-op, LFG, PvP, lore and targeted loot; other game channels stay separate so the server does not turn into one endless feed.

For a faster LFG post, include your platform, region, activity, difficulty and the time you want to start. Video requests and guide corrections go to the dedicated channels so they do not get lost in chat. The server is for ages 13 and over and follows Discord's Terms of Service.
