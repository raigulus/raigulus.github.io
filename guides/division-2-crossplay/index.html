Division 2 Crossplay Guide 2026: Platforms, Rollout & Cross-Progression

Unofficial fan guide. The Division 2 is getting full crossplay across PC, PlayStation, and Xbox in November 2026. This guide explains which platforms are supported today, how the rollout works, what changes for clans and matchmaking, and what cross-progression does and does not cover.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

What Crossplay Means in The Division 2

Crossplay lets players on different platforms matchmake together. In The Division 2 it is automatic and activated by default on the supported PC stores today, with full PC, PlayStation, and Xbox crossplay rolling out in November 2026. Crossplay covers PvE matchmaking, Conflict PvP, and Dark Zone activity.

Crossplay Rollout Timeline

  • Today (pre-rollout): PC crossplay across Ubisoft Connect, Steam, and Epic Games Store.
  • November 3, 2026: The Echoes of Central Park update adds the crossplay menus (inactive until migration).
  • November 10, 2026: PC players migrate to the new crossplay environment first. PC still matches PC only during this phase.
  • Mid-November 2026: PlayStation and Xbox players migrate once the environment is stable. Full crossplay across all three platforms goes live.
  • Goal: Complete rollout before Season 4 launches.

Cross-Progression: What Carries Over

The Division 2 supports automatic cross-progression between Epic, Steam, and Ubisoft Connect on PC. Your characters, gear, and progress sync across those PC storefronts through a single Ubisoft account. Cross-progression between consoles (PlayStation/Xbox) is not supported, and there is no cross-progression between PC and console.

Clan Changes With Crossplay

Once crossplay is live, clans become cross-platform. The clan capacity doubles from 50 to 100 members. Name conflicts between platforms are resolved during migration, and clan search works across all platforms.

What Does Not Carry Over

  • Cross-progression between PC and console is not supported.
  • Cross-progression between PlayStation and Xbox is not supported.
  • Platform-exclusive items may not transfer between storefronts.

How to Enable or Disable Crossplay

Crossplay is on by default. You can toggle it in the game settings under the gameplay or network section. Disabling crossplay limits matchmaking to your own platform, which may increase queue times.

FAQ

Does The Division 2 have crossplay?
Yes. PC crossplay across Ubisoft Connect, Steam, and Epic is live now. Full PC, PlayStation, and Xbox crossplay rolls out from November 10, 2026, with consoles joining once the environment is stable.
Does The Division 2 have cross-progression?
Yes, but only between PC storefronts (Epic, Steam, Ubisoft Connect). Console-to-console and PC-to-console cross-progression are not supported.
When does full crossplay go live?
PC migrates on November 10, 2026. PlayStation and Xbox follow in mid-November, with the goal of finishing before Season 4.
How many members can a clan have with crossplay?
Clan capacity doubles from 50 to 100 members once crossplay is live.
