The Division 2 / Release Date

Division 2 Release Date, Platforms & Editions

Unofficial fan guide. Tom Clancy's The Division 2 launched on March 15, 2019, for PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and PC (Ubisoft Connect and Epic Games Store). The game has since received multiple expansions, seasons, and updates, and remains actively supported.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Release Date

The Division 2 was released worldwide on March 15, 2019. Early access for Gold and Ultimate Edition owners began on March 12, 2019. The game launched simultaneously across all platforms.

Platforms

  • PlayStation 4 (playable on PS5 via backward compatibility)
  • Xbox One (playable on Xbox Series X|S via backward compatibility)
  • PC via Ubisoft Connect
  • PC via Epic Games Store
  • PC via Steam
  • Amazon Luna (cloud streaming)

Editions

  • Standard Edition: Base game.
  • Gold Edition: Base game + Year 1 Pass (early access to DLC episodes).
  • Ultimate Edition: Gold Edition + additional cosmetic and gear packs.
  • Warlords of New York Edition: Base game + Warlords of New York expansion.

System Requirements (PC)

MinimumWindows 10, Intel Core i5-2500K or AMD FX-6350, 8GB RAM, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 670 or AMD Radeon R9 270
RecommendedWindows 10, Intel Core i7-4790 or AMD Ryzen 5 1500X, 8GB RAM, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon RX 480

How to Play in 2026

The Division 2 is available on all major platforms and receives regular seasonal updates. New players can use the Level 40 boost to jump straight into endgame content. The game features a free trial on Ubisoft Connect and Steam.

FAQ

When was The Division 2 released?
The Division 2 launched on March 15, 2019, worldwide.
Is The Division 2 free to play?
No, but there is a free trial available on Ubisoft Connect and Steam.
Can I play The Division 2 on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S?
Yes, via backward compatibility. The game runs on both consoles.
Does The Division 2 have crossplay?
PC crossplay is live. Full PC, PlayStation, and Xbox crossplay rolls out from November 10, 2026.
