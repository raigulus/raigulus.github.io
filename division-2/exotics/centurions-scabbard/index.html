How to Get Centurion's Scabbard in The Division 2

Centurion's Scabbard is a melee-focused exotic holster that stacks increasing melee damage with each kill. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Centurion's Scabbard at a Glance

TypeExotic Holster
TalentBlade of Fury
SourceY6S2 Season Pass (Rank 29); Targeted Loot (Holster)

How to Unlock It

Originally available at Rank 29 on the Year 6 Season 2 Pass. Now available through Targeted Loot when Holsters are selected.

Blade of Fury, Explained

Blade of Fury grants +5% melee damage for 10 seconds on each kill, stacking up to 10 times for a maximum of +50% melee damage. The bonus encourages aggressive close-quarters play.

Build Tips

Best for melee builds, especially with the Firewall specialization and its shield. Combine with other melee damage bonuses for maximum effect.

FAQ

How many stacks can Blade of Fury reach?
Blade of Fury stacks up to 10 times, granting a maximum of +50% melee damage.
Do the stacks expire quickly?
Each stack lasts 10 seconds and refreshes on new kills. You need to keep killing to maintain maximum stacks.
Is this holster good for solo play?
Yes. The stacking melee damage makes solo content faster, especially on lower difficulties where enemies die quickly.
