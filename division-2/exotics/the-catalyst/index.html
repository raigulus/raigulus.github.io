How to Get The Catalyst in The Division 2

The Catalyst is a skill-focused exotic mask that stacks skill damage bonuses on kills. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The Catalyst at a Glance

TypeExotic Mask
TalentReaction
SourceBattle for Brooklyn final mission (random drop) or Brooklyn named bosses

How to Unlock It

The Catalyst drops from the final boss of the Battle for Brooklyn mission or from named bosses throughout Brooklyn. Run the mission on higher difficulties for better loot quality. Brooklyn named bosses also have a chance to drop it during free-roam activities.

Reaction, Explained

Reaction grants +10% skill damage for 10 seconds each time you get a kill, stacking up to 5 times for a maximum 50% skill damage bonus. Each new kill refreshes the duration of all active stacks. This rewards aggressive play and keeps your skills hitting harder the longer you stay in combat.

Build Tips

The Catalyst excels in skill DPS builds and hybrid setups that combine weapon kills with skill damage. Pair it with skills that benefit from raw damage boosts like the Striker Drone or Assault Turret. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Where does The Catalyst drop in Division 2?
The Catalyst drops from the Battle for Brooklyn final mission or from Brooklyn named bosses. It is a random drop with no guaranteed chance.
How many stacks can Reaction provide?
Reaction stacks up to 5 times, granting a total of 50% skill damage bonus. Each stack lasts 10 seconds and refreshes on new kills.
Is The Catalyst good for hybrid builds?
Yes, The Catalyst works well in hybrid builds that mix weapon damage with skill power. The stacking skill damage buff rewards aggressive playstyles.
