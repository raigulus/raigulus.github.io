How to Get Oxpecker in The Division 2

Oxpecker is an exotic SMG with the Symbiosis talent that provides healing to nearby allies on kills, making it a unique support weapon for group play. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Oxpecker at a Glance

TypeExotic SMG
TalentSymbiosis
SourceY6S3 Season Track (Rank 69), Targeted Loot (SMG)

How to Unlock It

Oxpecker was initially available as a guaranteed reward at Rank 69 on the Year 6 Season 3 track. After the season ended, it entered the general loot pool and can now drop from any targeted SMG source. Run Countdown with SMG targeting, clear Summit floors with SMG directives, or open Exotic Caches for a chance to obtain it.

Symbiosis, Explained

Symbiosis provides passive healing to all allies within 20 meters when you kill an enemy. Each kill grants +4% repair for 10 seconds, and the effect stacks up to 10 times. At maximum stacks, you are providing significant passive healing to your entire group just by getting kills. This makes Oxpecker uniquely valuable in group compositions where the healer also wants to contribute damage.

Build Tips

Oxpecker works best in support or crowd control builds that stay near teammates while dealing damage. A healing hybrid build that splits between skill tier and red cores lets you contribute both damage and sustained healing through Symbiosis. Pair it with a Tank or CC build to create a self-sustaining group that doesn't rely on a dedicated healer. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Can I still get Oxpecker after the season ends?
Yes, Oxpecker can drop from targeted SMG loot sources after the season ends. The Season Track at Rank 69 was the initial guaranteed source, but it enters the general loot pool afterward.
How much healing does Symbiosis provide?
Each kill grants +4% repair to all allies within 20 meters for 10 seconds. This stacks up to 10 times for a maximum 40% heal per stack tick to nearby allies.
Does Oxpecker heal myself too?
Yes, Symbiosis heals all allies within 20 meters, including yourself. You benefit from your own kill stacks just like your teammates do.
