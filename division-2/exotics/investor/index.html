How to Get Investor in The Division 2

Investor is an exotic mask that scales bonuses based on your non-Core Attribute colors. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Investor at a Glance

TypeExotic Mask
TalentSlotted
SourceY8S1 Season Track

How to Unlock It

Investor is a season track reward from Y8S1. Progress through the season pass by completing activities, projects, and league challenges. The mask unlocks at a specific track tier — check your season progress to see how far you need to go.

Slotted, Explained

Slotted grants bonuses for each non-Core Attribute on your gear based on its color. Red attributes give +10% Critical Hit Damage each, Yellow attributes give +5% Skill Efficiency each, and Blue attributes give +1% Armor Regeneration each. The more attributes of one color you stack, the stronger the corresponding bonus becomes.

Build Tips

Investor excels in attribute-stacking builds that commit to one color. A full red loadout maximizes crit damage, while yellow-heavy builds boost skill efficiency. Plan your gear around a single attribute color for the best results. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

What does the Slotted talent do?
Slotted grants bonuses based on non-Core Attribute colors: Red gives +10% Crit Damage, Yellow gives +5% Skill Efficiency, and Blue gives +1% Armor Regen per attribute.
How do you get the Investor mask?
Investor is obtained from the Y8S1 Season Track. Progress through the track to unlock it as a reward.
What builds work with Investor?
Investor excels in attribute-stacking builds that maximize a single attribute color. High-attribute loadouts with many red, yellow, or blue stats get the most value.
