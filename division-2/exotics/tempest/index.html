How to Get Tempest in The Division 2

Tempest rewards aggressive play with stacking critical hit damage buffs on every kill. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Tempest at a Glance

TypeExotic Pistol
TalentRestrained
SourceY7S2 Manhunt Climax guaranteed first Master clear.

How to Unlock It

Complete the Y7S2 Manhunt Climax mission on Master difficulty for your first clear. Tempest is guaranteed as a reward on your first successful Master run. After the first clear, subsequent Master completions have a chance to drop Tempest again if you need duplicates for recalibration.

Restrained, Explained

The moment your shield breaks, this full-auto pistol surges with bonus damage for a short window. A shield-player’s panic button, break, draw, delete.

Build Tips

Tempest is ideal for crit/pistol DPS builds that stack critical hit chance and critical hit damage attributes. Pair with gear sets like Striker or Hunter's Fury for additional damage multipliers. The more kills you chain, the more devastating Tempest becomes. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Restrained work?
Shield break triggers a brief damage surge on this full-auto 93R pistol.
Where does Tempest drop?
The Pact-season pistol is in targeted pistol loot and exotic caches.
