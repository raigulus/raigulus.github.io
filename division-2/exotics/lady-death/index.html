How to Get Lady Death in The Division 2

Lady Death is an exotic SMG that rewards movement with stacking damage bonuses through the Breathe Free talent, making it perfect for run-and-gun playstyles. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Lady Death at a Glance

TypeExotic SMG
TalentBreathe Free
SourceTargeted Loot (SMG), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Lady Death drops from any activity that awards exotic items, with targeted SMG loot sources providing the best farming efficiency. Run Countdown with SMG targeting, clear Summit floors with SMG directives, or open Exotic Caches from weekly projects. It has a standard exotic drop rate and can appear from any source that awards exotic items.

Breathe Free, Explained

Breathe Free builds stacks as you move around the map. Sprinting builds stacks faster than walking, and at 40 stacks, your next magazine deals massive bonus damage based on how far you traveled while building stacks. This creates a gameplay loop where you constantly reposition between magazine dumps, rewarding mobile players who don't stay in one spot too long.

Build Tips

Lady Death pairs perfectly with Hunter's Fury for close-quarters combat with disorient effects. Run-and-gun builds that prioritize mobility and speed synergize naturally with Breathe Free's movement requirement. Consider equipping gear with movement speed bonuses to build stacks faster between engagements. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How quickly do Breathe Free stacks build?
Breathe Free builds stacks passively as you move. Sprinting builds stacks faster than walking. At 40 stacks, your next magazine deals massive bonus damage based on total distance traveled.
Does Lady Death work with shielded enemies?
Yes, the bonus damage from Breathe Free applies to all bullets in the magazine, making it effective against shielded enemies when you have full stacks.
What is the best range for Lady Death?
Lady Death excels within 15 meters where SMG damage falloff is minimal. The run-and-gun playstyle required to build stacks naturally puts you in close quarters.
