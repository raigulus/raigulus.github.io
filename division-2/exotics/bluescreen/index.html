How to Get Bluescreen in The Division 2

The Bluescreen is an exotic LMG that marks enemies as you shoot them, then lets you trigger powerful crowd control effects by activating your skills. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Bluescreen at a Glance

TypeExotic LMG
TalentDisruptor
SourceCaptain Lewis Manhunt reward

How to Unlock It

Bluescreen is obtained by completing the Captain Lewis Manhunt, which becomes available during specific seasonal content periods. Complete all manhunt targets to unlock Captain Lewis as the final bounty, then defeat him to receive the Bluescreen. If the manhunt isn't currently active, check exotic caches from weekly projects as an alternative source.

Disruptor, Explained

Each bullet that hits an enemy adds a mark. At 5 marks, activating any skill triggers a disruption pulse that EMPs nearby enemies. At 10 marks, the trigger effect becomes disorientation, scrambling enemy aim. At 15 marks, the effect upgrades to foam that roots enemies in place. The marks reset after triggering, so the gameplay loop is: build marks by shooting, then time your skill activations for maximum crowd control impact.

Build Tips

Bluescreen pairs naturally with skill builds that activate abilities frequently. The Technician specialization's amplifier damage and the Survivalist's crossbow alt-weapon both complement the CC playstyle. Use the Banshee Pulse or Riot Foam chem launcher to chain crowd control effects. Hybrid builds with creeping death and status effect attributes amplify the disruption spread across enemy groups. FAQ

How many marks does Bluescreen need to trigger effects?
Bluescreen applies marks with each hit. At 5 marks, activating a skill triggers disruption. At 10 marks, it triggers disorientation. At 15 marks, it triggers foam that immobilizes enemies. Building marks quickly is key to maximizing its crowd control potential.
Can Bluescreen work with skill builds?
Absolutely. Bluescreen synergizes beautifully with skill builds since you activate skills frequently to trigger its effects. Pair it with the Technician or Survivalist specializations and use crowd control skills like the banshee pulse for maximum lockdown potential.
What is the best way to get Bluescreen?
Bluescreen is primarily obtained as a Captain Lewis Manhunt reward during seasonal manhunts. Check if the manhunt is currently available in your game. It can also occasionally appear in exotic caches from weekly projects.
