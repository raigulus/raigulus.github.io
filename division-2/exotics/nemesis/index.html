How to Get Nemesis in Division 2 [2026]

The Nemesis is the ultimate long-range exotic marksman rifle, rewarding patience with devastating charged shots that can one-shot elite enemies. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Nemesis at a Glance

TypeExotic MMR
TalentCounter-Sniper
SourceMulti-week quest (Tidal Basin + Invaded Strongholds + Grand Washington)

How to Unlock It

The Nemesis requires a multi-week quest chain. First, complete Tidal Basin on invaded difficulty to obtain the key piece. Then, over the following weeks, complete three different invaded strongholds (Grand Washington Hotel, Capitol Building, and Roosevelt Island) to collect the remaining parts. Once all pieces are gathered, return to Grand Washington Hotel to assemble the Nemesis at the crafting bench.

Counter-Sniper, Explained

Hold the trigger to charge up to double damage, release to fire — misses that still hit charge the next shot faster. Aiming marks targets through walls for up to +50% damage, and holstering it boosts your other scoped weapon’s headshots.

Build Tips

Nemesis is the cornerstone of one-shot sniper builds. Pair it with the Sharpshooter specialization for its headshot damage bonuses and stable bipod support. Stack headshot damage and weapon handling on your gear to maximize each charged shot. The Nemesis also works well as a secondary weapon for clearing distant targets while using another exotic as your primary. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How do the Nemesis talents work?
Counter-Sniper charges shots, the Nemesis mark tracks through walls up to +50% damage, and Preparation boosts holstered scoped headshots.
How do you get Nemesis?
A multi-week quest: an Adrestia rifle part plus three invaded-stronghold boss drops, then craft.
