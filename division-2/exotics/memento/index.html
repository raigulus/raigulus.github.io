How to Get Memento in The Division 2

Memento is the premier exotic backpack for solo play, stacking triple buffs from enemy trophies. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Memento at a Glance

TypeExotic Backpack
TalentKill Confirmed
SourceTargeted Loot (Backpack), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Memento drops from targeted loot when backpacks are the active slot or from exotic caches. Farm backpack targeted loot areas during global events for better chances. Open all weekly exotic caches from projects and SHD levels to increase your odds.

Kill Confirmed, Explained

Kill Confirmed causes enemies to drop trophies when killed. Walking over a trophy grants 3% bonus armor, 1% weapon damage, and 0.5% skill efficiency — each lasting 10 seconds. The duration of all active stacks refreshes every time you pick up a new trophy, so constant aggression keeps all buffs active indefinitely.

Build Tips

Memento is best-in-slot for solo builds across DPS, skill, and hybrid playstyles. It works with virtually any gear set since the triple buff adapts to your build. Prioritize killing enemies quickly to maintain trophy uptime. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Where does Memento drop in Division 2?
Memento drops from targeted loot (Backpack) or exotic caches. There is no specific mission that guarantees the drop.
How does Kill Confirmed work?
Enemies drop trophies on kill. Picking up a trophy grants 3% bonus armor, 1% weapon damage, and 0.5% skill efficiency for 10 seconds. All stacks refresh their duration when you grab another trophy.
Is Memento the best exotic backpack?
Memento is widely considered the best all-around exotic backpack for solo play. Its triple stat buff and stacking mechanic make it best-in-slot for most solo DPS and hybrid builds.
