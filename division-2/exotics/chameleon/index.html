How to Get Chameleon in The Division 2

Chameleon is a versatile exotic assault rifle that grants different buffs depending on where you land your shots, making it adaptable to any combat situation. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Chameleon at a Glance

TypeExotic Assault Rifle
TalentTraits
SourceTargeted Loot (AR), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Chameleon drops from any activity that awards exotic items, with the best odds coming from targeted assault rifle loot. Farm Countdown with AR targeting, run Summit floors with assault rifle directives, or open Exotic Caches from your weekly projects. It has a relatively generous drop rate compared to raid-locked exotics.

Traits, Explained

Traits gives Chameleon three distinct buffs based on where your bullets land. Headshots grant +20% critical hit chance for 45 seconds, body shots grant +20% weapon damage for 45 seconds, and leg shots grant +50% reload speed plus bonus ammunition. You can maintain multiple buffs simultaneously by alternating your aim, creating a dynamic playstyle that adapts to whatever you need most in the moment.

Build Tips

Chameleon's versatility means it works with virtually any DPS build. The crit chance buff from headshots stacks naturally with crit-focused gear sets, while the weapon damage buff makes it viable even in pure red builds. Try pairing it with a secondary that covers its weaknesses, or go all-in on the flexible playstyle with a balanced loadout. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Which body part should I aim for with Chameleon?
Headshots grant crit chance, body shots grant weapon damage, and leg shots grant reload speed. Prioritize based on your current need: crit chance for sustained DPS, weapon damage for burst, or reload speed for uptime.
How long do Traits buffs last?
Each buff from Traits lasts 45 seconds. You can maintain multiple buffs simultaneously by alternating your aim between different body parts.
Is Chameleon better than other exotic ARs?
Chameleon is one of the most versatile exotics because it provides three different bonuses. While it may not excel in a single area like Eagle Bearer or St. Elmo's Engine, its flexibility makes it reliable in any situation.
