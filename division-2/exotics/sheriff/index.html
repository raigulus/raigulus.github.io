How to Get Sheriff in Division 2 [2026]

The Sheriff is a high-impact exotic shotgun that rewards aggressive play with stacking damage buffs through its Lawman talent. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sheriff at a Glance

TypeExotic Shotgun
TalentLawman
SourceY7S3 Manhunt Climax (Master first clear guaranteed)

How to Unlock It

The Sheriff is guaranteed as a first-time clear reward from the Y7S3 Manhunt Climax mission completed on Master difficulty. This is the only guaranteed way to obtain it initially. After your first Master clear, Sheriff can also drop from targeted loot shotgun zones and exotic caches, though with lower odds. Completing the Manhunt on Master should be your first priority.

Lawman, Explained

The Lawman talent grants you a stacking damage buff each time you eliminate an enemy. Each kill increases your damage output, and the stacks persist for a limited duration. The more kills you chain together, the more powerful each subsequent shot becomes. This creates a snowball effect where clearing groups of enemies makes you progressively deadlier.

Build Tips

Sheriff pairs perfectly with Striker gear sets for maximum damage stacking. The combination of Striker's RPM buffs and Lawman's damage amplification creates devastating burst potential. DPS-focused builds with high critical hit chance will maximize the value of each Lawman stack. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

What does the Lawman talent do on Sheriff?
Lawman grants a stacking damage buff each time you kill an enemy. The buff increases your damage output significantly as you chain kills together, rewarding aggressive play.
How do I get the Sheriff exotic shotgun?
Sheriff is guaranteed as a first-time clear reward from the Y7S3 Manhunt Climax mission on Master difficulty. After your first clear, it can drop from targeted loot shotgun zones and exotic caches.
Is Sheriff good with Striker builds?
Yes, Sheriff pairs exceptionally well with Striker gear sets because the Lawman talent stacks multiply with Striker's existing damage bonuses, creating devastating burst damage.
