How to Get Vertigo in Division 2 [2026]

Vertigo is the exotic GR9 light machine gun from Red Moon Rising. Its Startling talent turns every suppression into Disorient plus team-wide damage to targets out of cover, rewarding the hold-the-trigger LMG playstyle. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Vertigo at a Glance

TypeExotic LMG (HK GR9)
TalentStartling
SourceRed Moon Rising reward pools

How to Unlock It

Vertigo drops from the dedicated Red Moon Rising reward pools during the Y8S3.1 event - farm the PvE Dark Zone event pools while the event is live. See the Red Moon Rising guide for the event window and pool details.

Startling, Explained

Whenever Vertigo suppresses an enemy, that enemy takes Disorient, and you and your allies gain +30% damage to targets out of cover for 15 seconds. The buff refreshes with continued pressure, so sustained fire on grouped enemies keeps the whole squad amplified. Suppression is the trigger, not kills - volume of fire is the build.

Build Tips

Vertigo runs at 750 RPM with a 200-round belt, 35m optimal range and 65% headshot damage, and every attachment comes fixed: +15% crit chance optics, +50 round magazine, +15% reload underbarrel and +10% rate of fire muzzle. The weapon cores are fixed LMG damage and damage to targets out of cover, which stacks directly with the Startling buff.
