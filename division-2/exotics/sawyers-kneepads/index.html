The Division 2 / Exotic Kneepads

How to Get Sawyer's Kneepads in The Division 2

Sawyer's Kneepads are exotic kneepads that reward a stationary playstyle with massive damage bonuses. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sawyer's Kneepads at a Glance

TypeExotic Kneepads
TalentUnbreakable
SourceHyena named boss drops; Targeted Loot (Kneepads)

How to Unlock It

Farm Hyena named bosses in the open world or Jefferson Trade Center. You can also target Kneepads as Targeted Loot in any mission or activity.

Unbreakable, Explained

Unbreakable prevents stagger and grants +25% weapon damage after remaining stationary for 3 seconds. The bonus persists as long as you stay still, making it ideal for LMG turret setups.

Build Tips

Pair with an LMG and Fixed Cannon Turret for stationary DPS builds. Striker or Hunter's Fury sets work well when holding a fixed position.

FAQ

Can Sawyer's Kneepads drop from any difficulty?
Yes. They can drop on any difficulty, but targeted loot and higher difficulties increase your odds significantly.
Do you need to own Warlords of New York?
No. Sawyer's Kneepads are a base game exotic and can be farmed without the expansion.
What is the best mission to farm them?
Jefferson Trade Center is the most efficient farm when Hyena targeted loot is active, as it features multiple named Hyena enemies.
