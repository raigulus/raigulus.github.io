The Division 2 / Exotic Kneepads

How to Get Sawyer's Kneepads in The Division 2

Sawyer's Kneepads are exotic kneepads that reward a stationary playstyle with massive damage bonuses. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sawyer's Kneepads at a Glance

TypeExotic Kneepads
TalentStand Your Ground
SourceHyena named boss drops; Targeted Loot (Kneepads)

How to Unlock It

Farm Hyena named bosses in the open world or Jefferson Trade Center. You can also target Kneepads as Targeted Loot in any mission or activity.

Stand Your Ground, Explained

Explosions cannot stagger you, and every motionless second stacks +3% weapon damage up to ten — start moving and the pile melts away. A cover-camper’s dream and a rusher’s trap.

Build Tips

Pair with an LMG and Fixed Cannon Turret for stationary DPS builds. Striker or Hunter's Fury sets work well when holding a fixed position.

FAQ

How does Stand Your Ground work?
Immune to explosion stagger; +3% weapon damage per still second to 10 stacks, lost on moving.
Where do Sawyer’s Kneepads drop?
The Episode 2 kneepads are available from targeted kneepads loot and exotic caches.
