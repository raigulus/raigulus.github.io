How to Get Prima Donna in Division 2 [2026]

Prima Donna is a patient marksman's exotic MMR that rewards staying out of combat with massive damage stacks and armor sustain. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Prima Donna at a Glance

TypeExotic MMR
TalentOut of Combat
SourceParadise Lost Incursion (weekly lockout)

How to Unlock It

Prima Donna is exclusive to the Paradise Lost Incursion and cannot be obtained anywhere else. Complete the incursion on any difficulty for a chance at the drop, but be aware there is a weekly lockout — you only get one chance per week. This makes Prima Donna one of the rarer exotics in the game, requiring consistent weekly clears over time.

Out of Combat, Explained

Out of Combat builds up to 10 stacks while you are not actively engaged in combat. Each stack provides +12.5% amplified damage, meaning at full stacks you deal 125% bonus damage. When you kill an enemy, one stack is consumed to repair 3% of your armor. This creates a playstyle where you carefully position, take your shots, then pull back to rebuild stacks before engaging again.

Build Tips

Prima Donna excels in sniper-support hybrid builds. Position at long range, eliminate priority targets with your damage stacks, then fall back to let stacks rebuild. The armor repair on kill provides valuable self-sustain without needing healing skills. Pair with a cooldown reduction build to keep your skills active while you reposition.
