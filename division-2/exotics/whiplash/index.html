The Division 2 / Exotic Pistol

How to Get Whiplash in The Division 2

Whiplash rewards weapon swapping with auto-reloads and massive damage buffs, perfect for burst DPS rotations. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Whiplash at a Glance

TypeExotic Pistol
TalentFaster Than Reloading — Holster for 5s to prime; swapping reloads Primary and Secondary; +20% ROF and +50% weapon damage for 15s.
SourcePentagon Climax Mission guaranteed first Master clear.

How to Unlock It

Complete the Pentagon Climax Mission on Master difficulty for your first clear. Whiplash is guaranteed as a reward on your first successful Master completion. After the first clear, subsequent Master runs have a chance to drop additional Whiplash copies for recalibration or sharing.

Faster Than Reloading, Explained

Holster Whiplash for 5 seconds to activate the prime effect. When you swap to your Primary or Secondary weapon, both weapons instantly reload and you gain +20% rate of fire and +50% weapon damage for 15 seconds. This encourages a weapon-swap playstyle where you prime Whiplash, swap to your main weapon for the buff duration, then swap back to re-prime.

Build Tips

Whiplash excels in weapon-swap burst builds that capitalize on the 15-second damage window. Pair with high-RPM weapons to maximize the ROF bonus, or use slow hard-hitting weapons to benefit most from the +50% damage. talents like Optimist or Damage to Armor synergize well. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How long do I need to holster Whiplash to prime it?
You need to holster Whiplash for 5 seconds to activate the prime effect. A visual indicator shows when the weapon is ready to swap for the reload and damage buff.
Does the ROF bonus apply to both weapons?
Yes, the 20% rate of fire bonus applies to your Primary and Secondary weapons after swapping from the primed Whiplash. It does not affect the pistol itself.
Can Whiplash's damage buff be refreshed early?
No, the 15-second buff duration is fixed. You must wait for it to expire before holstering Whiplash again to re-prime the effect.
