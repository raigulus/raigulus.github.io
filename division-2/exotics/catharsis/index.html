How to Get Catharsis in The Division 2

Catharsis is a tank-oriented exotic mask that turns incoming damage into bonus armor and full heals. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Catharsis at a Glance

TypeExotic Mask
TalentPurgatory
SourceRequires blueprint first (exotic cache), then Targeted Loot (Mask)

How to Unlock It

You must obtain the Catharsis blueprint before it can drop as a regular item. The blueprint comes from exotic caches. Once learned, farm targeted loot in mask slots for a chance at the item itself. This two-step process means patience is required.

Purgatory, Explained

Purgatory builds stacks each time you take damage, capping at 50. Once you reach 50 stacks, your next outgoing hit activates 50% bonus armor for 10 seconds. When that bonus armor depletes, your entire base armor bar is instantly repaired. This creates a powerful loop of absorbing hits and resetting your health pool.

Build Tips

Catharsis pairs excellently with Foundry Bulwark and other tank-focused gear sets. It excels in survivability builds where you intentionally take damage to trigger the heal cycle. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Do you need the blueprint for Catharsis?
Yes, you need the Catharsis blueprint first. It drops from exotic caches. Once you have the blueprint, Catharsis can drop from targeted loot (Mask).
How does the Purgatory talent work?
Taking damage builds stacks up to 50. At 50 stacks your next hit grants 50% bonus armor for 10 seconds. When that bonus armor breaks, it fully repairs your base armor.
Is Catharsis good for solo play?
Yes, Catharsis is excellent for solo play. The bonus armor and full heal provide strong survivability without needing group support.
