How to Get Fafnir in The Division 2

Fafnir is the Red Horizon exotic shotgun. It unlocks on the free Season Pass track by playing through the season — no purchase needed. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Fafnir at a Glance

TypeExotic Shotgun
TalentDragon's Breath
SourceFree Season Pass track (Red Horizon)
Fixed mods+15% crit chance, +5 rounds, +10% handling

How to Unlock It

Progress the Red Horizon season on any character and claim the Fafnir reward tier on the free track. Because it is a guaranteed season reward rather than a random drop, there is no farm route — seasonal activity, projects and XP all push the track forward.

Dragon's Breath, Explained

Each shot has a 40% chance to apply Burn, and weapon damage scales with half of your Status Effect bonus. Two practical notes: Burn still suffers normal Status Effect diminishing returns on enemies, so the real-world rate feels lower than 40% on tough targets; and the damage scaling rewards stacking Status Effects, which makes fire-oriented brand sets and the build planner your friends.

Build Tips

Pair Fafnir with Status Effect pieces and close-range content (Countdown, open-world bounties, legendary pushes). The fixed +15% crit chance frees one mod slot worth of crit elsewhere. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How do you get the Fafnir exotic shotgun?
Fafnir unlocks on the free Red Horizon Season Pass track by playing through the season. No purchase is needed.
What does the Dragon's Breath talent do?
Each shot has a 40% chance to apply Burn, and weapon damage scales with half of your Status Effect bonus.
What are Fafnir's fixed mods?
15% critical hit chance on optics, 5 extra rounds in the magazine, and 10% weapon handling on the underbarrel.
