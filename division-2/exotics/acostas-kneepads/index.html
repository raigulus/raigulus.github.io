How to Get Acosta's Kneepads in The Division 2

Acosta's Kneepads reward cover-to-cover movement with a weapon damage buff that encourages tactical repositioning. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Acosta's Kneepads at a Glance

TypeExotic Kneepads
TalentEscape Plan
SourceTargeted Loot (Kneepads); Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Farm Targeted Loot for Kneepads in any mission or open world zone. They also drop from Exotic Caches andseasonal rewards.

Escape Plan, Explained

Vaulting, climbing or dropping boosts movement speed, standing still banks even more, and status effects speed you up instead of slowing you down — with halved movement impairment throughout.

Build Tips

Pair with mobile DPS builds that already use cover-to-cover moves frequently. Works well with assault rifle or LMG builds in Heroic content.

FAQ

How does Escape Plan work?
Movement actions grant +10% speed, stillness banks +20%, status adds +10% up to 20% total, impairment halved.
Where do Acosta’s Kneepads drop?
The First Rogue season pass reward, now in targeted kneepads loot and caches.
