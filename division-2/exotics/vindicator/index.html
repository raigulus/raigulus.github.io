The Division 2 / Exotic Rifle

How to Get Vindicator in The Division 2

The Vindicator is an exotic rifle that lets you mark an enemy and then deliver a single devastating amplified shot for massive burst damage. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Vindicator at a Glance

TypeExotic Rifle
TalentMark and Execute
SourceDescent Mode vendor (350 NSA Credits)

How to Unlock It

Vindicator is purchased from the Descent Mode vendor using NSA Credits. Enter Descent from the game mode selection menu and locate the vendor near the entrance area. You need 350 NSA Credits, which are earned by completing rounds in Descent mode. The further you progress in Descent, the more NSA Credits you earn per run, so improving your Descent performance speeds up the farming process.

Mark and Execute, Explained

Mark and Execute works in two stages. First, aim at an enemy briefly to apply a mark, which appears as a visible indicator on the target. Once marked, your next shot against that enemy deals 100% amplified damage, effectively doubling the hit. The mark has a limited duration, so you need to fire quickly after marking. This creates a satisfying rhythm of mark-then-execute that rewards careful target selection and precise timing, especially against elite and named enemies.

Build Tips

Vindicator is purpose-built for one-shot and burst DPS builds. The Sharpshooter specialization's headshot damage bonuses and stability improvements complement the mark-and-execute playstyle. Equip Airaldi Holdings for bonus marksman damage and Providence Defense for headshot and critical hit damage. In PvP, Vindicator excels at ambush tactics where a single amplified headshot can eliminate unsuspecting players. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How much bonus damage does Mark and Execute deal?
Mark and Execute amplifies the next shot's damage by 100%, effectively doubling it. The mark is applied by aiming at an enemy for a brief moment, and the amplified shot must land within a short window. This makes Vindicator ideal for burst DPS against high-value targets.
Where is the Descent Mode vendor?
The Descent Mode vendor is located in the Descent game mode lobby area. Access Descent from the game mode selection menu, then look for the NSA Credits vendor near the entrance. You need 350 NSA Credits to purchase Vindicator, earned by completing Descent rounds.
Can Vindicator be used for one-shot builds?
Yes, Vindicator excels in one-shot builds. The 100% amplified damage from Mark and Execute, combined with headshot damage bonuses and the Sharpshooter specialization, can eliminate most non-boss enemies in a single shot. It's particularly effective in Dark Zone PvP for surprise ambushes.
