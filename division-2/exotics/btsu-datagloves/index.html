How to Get BTSU Datagloves in Division 2 [2026]

BTSU Datagloves are exotic gloves that supercharge your skills with a powerful overcharge effect on kills. They are a staple of endgame skill builds in The Division 2. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

BTSU Datagloves at a Glance

TypeExotic Gloves
TalentInfused
SourceBlack Tusk boss drops; Targeted Loot (Gloves)

How to Unlock It

BTSU Datagloves drop from Black Tusk named bosses found in the open world and in Black Tusk-controlled missions like Tidal Basin and Roosevelt Island. Setting Targeted Loot to Gloves in Black Tusk zones improves your farming efficiency. You can also earn them from Exotic Caches obtained through weekly projects, season rewards, and Summit completions.

Infused, Explained

Infused grants a passive +15% skill damage bonus at all times. When you kill an enemy, both of your skills become overcharged for 10 seconds. Overcharged skills operate at elevated tier levels, meaning your Hive lasts longer, your Seeker Mines deal more damage, and your Chem Launcher effects are amplified. The overcharge window rewards you for chaining kills together in rapid succession.

Build Tips

BTSU Datagloves are the backbone of Hive and Seeker Mine skill builds. Pair them with Hard Wired for skill cooldown reduction or Empress International for additional skill damage and efficiency. The Tech Specialist specialization adds even more skill tier bonuses. Deploy your Hive for area control and use Seeker Mines to chain kills that keep your overcharge active. FAQ

What does the Infused talent do on BTSU Datagloves?
Infused provides a passive +15% skill damage bonus. When you kill an enemy, your skills become overcharged for 10 seconds, significantly boosting their effectiveness. This makes the gloves extremely powerful for skill builds that can chain kills together.
Where do BTSU Datagloves drop in Division 2?
BTSU Datagloves drop from Black Tusk named bosses in the open world and missions. You can also get them from Targeted Loot when Gloves is the targeted slot, or from Exotic Caches. Farming Tidal Basin or Roosevelt Island missions increases your chances.
What is the best skill build for BTSU Datagloves in Division 2?
BTSU Datagloves excel on Hive and Seeker Mine builds. The overcharge effect from Infused makes your Hive last longer and Seeker Mines deal massive damage. Hard Wired or Empress International gear sets provide additional skill bonuses that stack with the gloves.
