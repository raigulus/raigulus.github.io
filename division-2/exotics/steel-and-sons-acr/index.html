How to Get Steel & Sons ACR in Division 2 [2026]

Steel & Sons ACR is the exotic rifle reward from the Broken Rain Incursion. First teased simply as the exotic ACR before its full Steel & Sons name was revealed. Its Confirm and Execute talent stacks amplified damage on body shots and cashes out on headshots, rewarding disciplined aim at long range. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

How to Unlock It

Steel & Sons ACR is a reward from the Broken Rain Incursion. Clear the incursion with a squad - see the Broken Rain Incursion guide for boss mechanics and the Day One clear. The incursion key chest resets weekly, so plan repeat clears around the reset.

Confirm and Execute, Explained

Body shots add one stack to the enemy for 10 seconds, up to 4 stacks, and each stack gives +4% amplified damage. Hitting a different enemy removes all stacks, so stay on target. Your next headshot consumes all stacks for +11% amplified damage per stack. Destroying an enemy weakpoint makes them take +30% amplified damage from you for 5 seconds, which pairs perfectly with the cash-out headshot.

Build Tips

The ACR runs at 420 RPM with a 45m optimal range and 60% headshot damage, so it plays like a precision rifle with a rifle magazine. Stack rifle damage and critical-hit damage - the rifle cores come fixed on the weapon. Aim for weakpoints first for the +30% window, build 4 stacks, then cash out with a headshot.
