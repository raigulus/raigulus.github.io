How to Get Iron Will in Division 2 [2026]

Iron Will is an exotic chest piece that rewards precision marksmanship by converting body shots into headshots. It is a top-tier pick for sharpshooter builds in The Division 2. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

How to Unlock It

Iron Will is a reward from the Year 8 Season 3 free season pass track. To unlock it you need to earn experience points by completing missions, activities, bounties, and weekly projects. Using directives on difficulty settings accelerates your XP gain. Once you reach the required tier on the free track, Iron Will is added to your inventory automatically.

Resolved, Explained

Resolved makes your next body shot count as a headshot after aiming down sights. This effect has a 1-second cooldown in PvE and a 2-second cooldown in PvP. The talent only works with marksman rifles, rifles, or pistols, so it is designed for precision-focused playstyles. Landing body shots becomes nearly as rewarding as headshots, which is especially useful against enemies with small or hard-to-hit head hitboxes.

FAQ

What weapons work with Iron Will in Division 2?
Iron Will's Resolved talent only activates when using a marksman rifle, rifle, or pistol. Assault rifles, SMGs, LMGs, and shotguns will not trigger the body-shot-as-headshot effect, so you need to build around precision weapons.
How do I get Iron Will in Division 2?
Iron Will is obtained from the Year 8 Season 3 free reward track. You must progress through the season pass by earning XP from activities, projects, and directives to reach the tier where Iron Will is awarded.
Does Iron Will work in PvP in Division 2?
Yes, but the cooldown is longer in PvP. In the Dark Zone or Conflict, Resolved has a 2-second cooldown instead of the 1-second PvE cooldown. The talent still converts body shots to headshots, making it viable for sharpshooter PvP builds.
