How to Get Exodus in Division 2 [2026]

Exodus are exotic gloves that boost your mobility and hazard protection when you roll, making them a strong choice for agents who value speed and survival in The Division 2. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Exodus at a Glance

TypeExotic Gloves
TalentSmoke Screen
SourceY6S3 Season Pass (Rank 29); Targeted Loot (Gloves)

How to Unlock It

Exodus is primarily obtained from the Year 6 Season 3 free reward track. You need to reach Rank 29 on the free track by earning XP from missions, activities, and weekly projects. After the season ends, Exodus can also drop from Targeted Loot when Gloves is the targeted slot, and from Exotic Caches earned through various endgame activities.

Smoke Screen, Explained

When your armor breaks, a smoke bomb hides you from enemies for three seconds on a forty-second loop — in PvP it also grants pulse resistance and hides your nameplate. The great escape, on cooldown.

Build Tips

Exodus pairs well with mobile DPS builds that use frequent repositioning. The Gunner specialization provides ammo regeneration for sustained fire between rolls. Negotiator's Dilemma or Hunter's Fury gear sets complement the aggressive movement style. In PvP, the hazard protection is particularly valuable against bleed and burn builds commonly found in the Dark Zone. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Smoke Screen work?
Armor break deploys concealing smoke for 3s in PvE, plus pulse resist in PvP. 40s cooldown.
Where do Exodus gloves drop?
Season track rank reward, then targeted gloves loot and caches.
