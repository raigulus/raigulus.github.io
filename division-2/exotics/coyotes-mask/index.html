How to Get Coyote's Mask in The Division 2

Coyote's Mask is a top-tier exotic mask that buffs your entire group based on enemy distance. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Coyote's Mask at a Glance

TypeExotic Mask
TalentPack Leader
SourceJefferson Trade Center final boss (random drop)

How to Unlock It

Coyote's Mask drops exclusively from the final boss encounter at Jefferson Trade Center. Run the mission on any difficulty — Challenging or Heroic recommended for better loot quality overall. The drop is random with no pity timer, so expect multiple runs before it lands.

Pack Leader, Explained

Pack Leader provides distance-based buffs to you and nearby allies. Enemies within 15 meters grant critical hit damage, those between 15 and 25 meters grant critical hit chance, and anything beyond 25 meters grants a raw damage bonus. The effect dynamically switches based on your positioning relative to targets.

Build Tips

Coyote's Mask is best-in-slot for group DPS compositions, particularly Negotiator's Dilemma builds. Pair it with high crit chance gear to maximize the close-range damage buff. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Where does Coyote's Mask drop in Division 2?
Coyote's Mask drops from the final boss at Jefferson Trade Center on any difficulty. It is a random drop with no guaranteed chance.
What is the best build for Coyote's Mask?
Coyote's Mask excels in group DPS builds, especially Negotiator's Dilemma setups. It provides universal crit buffs that benefit your entire team.
Can Coyote's Mask drop on higher difficulties?
Yes, Coyote's Mask can drop on any difficulty level from Challenging to Heroic. Higher difficulties do not increase the drop rate.
