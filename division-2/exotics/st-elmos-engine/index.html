How to Get St. Elmo's Engine in The Division 2

St. Elmo's Engine is a powerful exotic assault rifle that builds Shock stacks with each hit, eventually loading your magazine with devastating Shock ammo. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

St. Elmo's Engine at a Glance

TypeExotic Assault Rifle
TalentActum Est
SourceTargeted Loot (AR), Countdown, Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

St. Elmo's Engine drops from any activity that can award exotic items, but targeting assault rifle loot sources gives you the best odds. Run Countdown with assault rifle targeted loot, complete Summit floors with AR directives, or open Exotic Caches from weekly projects. The drop rate is random, so patience is key.

Actum Est, Explained

Each bullet you land applies a Shock stack to the target. Once you reach 10 stacks, your next magazine is automatically loaded with Shock ammo. Shock ammo stuns enemies on hit, making it invaluable for controlling rushers and heavies. The stacks persist across magazine swaps, so you can build toward Shock ammo even during reloads.

Build Tips

St. Elmo's Engine shines in crowd control builds that leverage the Shock ammo for area denial. Pair it with Negotiator's Dilemma for multi-target pressure or Hunter's Fury for aggressive close-quarters play. The Shock effect scales well with damage-focused builds since stunned enemies can't fight back. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Can St. Elmo's Engine drop from any activity?
St. Elmo's Engine can drop from any activity that awards exotic items, but it is most efficiently farmed from targeted assault rifle loot sources like Countdown or Summit.
How many Shock stacks does Actum Est apply?
Each hit applies one Shock stack. At 10 stacks, your next magazine is loaded with Shock ammo that stuns enemies on contact.
Is St. Elmo's Engine good for solo play?
Yes, the Shock ammo mechanic makes it excellent for solo play since it can stun lock targets, giving you breathing room against aggressive enemies.
