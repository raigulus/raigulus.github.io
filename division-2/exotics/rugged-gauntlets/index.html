How to Get Rugged Gauntlets in Division 2 [2026]

Rugged Gauntlets are exotic gloves that restore armor on every kill, giving you consistent sustain during extended firefights in The Division 2. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Rugged Gauntlets at a Glance

TypeExotic Gloves
TalentIron Grip
SourceTargeted Loot (Gloves), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Rugged Gauntlets drop from any source that awards exotic gloves. The most effective farming method is setting Targeted Loot to Gloves and running missions or farming open-world bosses in the targeted zone. Countdown and Summit floor 100 provide good opportunities as well. Exotic Caches from weekly SHD level projects, season pass milestones, and Descent Nemesis kills can also randomly drop Rugged Gauntlets.

Iron Grip, Explained

Halves recoil penalties when hip-firing or blind-firing from cover — for agents who never slow down to aim.

Build Tips

Rugged Gauntlets are ideal for tank and survivability builds that want to minimize reliance on healing skills. Pair them with Foundry Bulwark or Gila Guard gear sets for maximum armor values. The Crusader shield or Bulwark shield synergize well since you can push aggressively while benefiting from armor on kill. The Survivalist specialization adds additional healing bonuses.
