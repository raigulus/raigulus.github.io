How to Get NinjaBike Messenger Kneepads in The Division 2

NinjaBike Messenger Kneepads reward aggressive movement with instant reloads and bonus armor on every cover-to-cover move. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

NinjaBike Messenger Kneepads at a Glance

TypeExotic Kneepads
TalentEvasive
SourceTargeted Loot (Kneepads); Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Farm Targeted Loot for Kneepads in any mission or open world zone. They also drop from Exotic Caches earned through Weekly Projects and Summit.

Evasive, Explained

Evasive reloads all your weapons and grants 20% bonus armor for 5 seconds each time you perform a cover-to-cover move. This encourages constant repositioning during combat.

Build Tips

Excellent for mobile DPS builds that rely on SMGs or assault rifles. Pair with a red core Striker build for aggressive playstyle. See tested builds at /division-2/builds/.

FAQ

Does Evasive trigger on mantle moves?
No. Evasive only activates on deliberate cover-to-cover moves, not on mantling or climbing.
How long does the bonus armor last?
The 20% bonus armor lasts for 5 seconds after each cover-to-cover move.
Are these kneepads good for solo play?
Yes. The bonus armor and instant reloads make solo content much easier by keeping you alive and loaded during aggressive pushes.
