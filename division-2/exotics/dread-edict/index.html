How to Get Dread Edict in Division 2 [2026]

The Dread Edict exotic MMR amplifies headshot damage while scoped, rewarding steady aim and consistent precision from long range. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Dread Edict at a Glance

TypeExotic MMR
TalentFull Stop
SourceTargeted Loot (MMR), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Dread Edict drops randomly from targeted loot MMR areas and exotic caches. There is no quest chain or specific mission required. Farm missions with MMR targeted loot on Heroic or Legendary difficulty for the best drop rates. Exotic Caches from weekly projects and season rewards also have a chance to contain this weapon.

Full Stop, Explained

Body shots stack damage up to twenty — headshots count double. Reloading cashes the pile into temporary armor, while headshot kills refill the magazine for free without counting as a reload.

Build Tips

Dread Edict is designed for scoped sniper builds that prioritize steady, sustained damage. Pair it with the Sharpshooter specialization for its stability bonuses and headshot damage perks. Stack headshot damage, weapon handling, and scope-related bonuses on your gear. The talent rewards patience — hold your scope, line up headshots, and watch the damage multiply.
