The Division 2 / Exotic Holster

How to Get Waveform in The Division 2

Waveform is a hybrid exotic holster that cycles weapon damage bonuses between your primary and secondary weapons while boosting skills. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Waveform at a Glance

TypeExotic Holster
TalentAlternating Current
SourceTargeted Loot (Holster); Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Farm Targeted Loot for Holsters in any mission or open world zone. They also drop from Exotic Caches earned through weekly projects.

Alternating Current, Explained

Alternating Current cycles +10% bonus damage between your primary and secondary weapons every 10 seconds. Additionally, it provides a flat +10% skill damage bonus at all times.

Build Tips

Perfect for hybrid skill and weapon builds. Equip two different weapon types and switch to whichever has the active bonus. See tested builds at /division-2/builds/.

FAQ

How do I know which weapon has the bonus?
A visual indicator on your HUD shows which weapon currently has the +10% damage bonus from Alternating Current.
Does the skill damage bonus stack with other talents?
Yes. The 10% skill damage from Waveform stacks with other skill damage bonuses from gear, talents, and SHD watch.
What weapons work best with Waveform?
Pair two different weapon types like an assault rifle and SMG, or LMG and shotgun, so you can maximize the cycling bonus.
