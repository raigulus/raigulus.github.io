How to Get Busy Little Bee in The Division 2

Busy Little Bee marks enemies for amplified damage, rewarding quick weapon swaps with devastating burst damage. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Busy Little Bee at a Glance

TypeExotic Pistol
TalentBusy Little Bee
SourceTargeted Loot (Pistol), Exotic Caches.

How to Unlock It

Set your targeted loot to Pistols and farm Heroic missions, control points, or bounties for a chance at the drop. Alternatively, open Exotic Caches from the Seasonal Vendor or Raids. There is no quest requirement, it is a random world drop once you have the targeted loot set correctly.

Busy Little Bee, Explained

Tag a different enemy per shot to stack damage up to ten, then swap weapons to cash the stacks in for ten seconds of pain. Swapping again early cancels the buff.

Build Tips

Busy Little Bee excels in sidearm-swapping DPS builds. Equip it alongside your primary weapon and swap frequently during engagements to keep marks active. Pair withdamage-boosting talents like Flatline or Spike for maximum burst potential. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Busy Little Bee work?
Each shot on a different target builds one stack up to ten for +20% weapon damage each. Swap weapons to activate the stacks for ten seconds.
Where does Busy Little Bee drop?
It is a Season 10 exotic available from targeted pistol loot and exotic caches.
