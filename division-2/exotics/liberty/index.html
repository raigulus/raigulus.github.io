How to Get Liberty in The Division 2

Liberty is an exotic pistol that rewards headshot kills with shield repairs and devastating bonus damage at full stacks. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Liberty at a Glance

TypeExotic Pistol
TalentBreath — Headshot kills repair your shield (3% per stack, max 30); at 10 stacks, deal 500% bonus damage.
SourceMulti-part quest: Capitol Building (Pistol), American History Museum (Parts), Viewpoint Museum (Assembly), Space Admin HQ (Blueprint).

How to Unlock It

Collect four quest components across Washington D.C. missions. First, get the High-Power Pistol from Capitol Building on Hard or above. Second, find the weapon parts in American History Museum. Third, retrieve the assembly from Viewpoint Museum. Fourth, grab the blueprint from Space Administration HQ. Take all four parts to Inaya at the White House to craft Liberty.

Breath, Explained

Each headshot kill with Liberty grants a stack of Breath. Each stack repairs your equipped shield by 3%, up to a maximum of 30% total repair. Once you reach 10 stacks, your next shot deals 500% bonus weapon damage, then the stacks reset. This makes Liberty ideal for shield-based builds that value sustained pistol fire.

Build Tips

Liberty pairs perfectly with the Crusader or Bulwark Shield, letting you tank damage while building stacks. Stack armor and shield health to maximize survivability while building to 10 stacks for the big hit. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

What difficulty do I need to complete the Liberty quest missions on?
You can complete all four quest missions on any difficulty including Story mode. The drops are guaranteed regardless of difficulty setting.
Can Liberty drops be shared with teammates?
No, all four Liberty quest components are personal drops. You must collect each part yourself during your own mission runs.
How many stacks do I need for Liberty's full damage bonus?
You need 10 stacks of Breath to unlock the 500% bonus damage effect. Each headshot kill grants 1 stack, and stacks also repair your shield.
