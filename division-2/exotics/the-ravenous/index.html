How to Get The Ravenous in The Division 2

The Ravenous is an exotic rifle that uses a unique prime-and-detonate mechanic, where left-click primes enemies and right-click triggers devastating bonus damage explosions. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The Ravenous at a Glance

TypeExotic Rifle
TalentGeri and Freki
SourceIron Horse Raid exclusive (weekly lockout)

How to Unlock It

The Ravenous is a guaranteed drop from the Iron Horse Raid boss, making it one of the raid-exclusive exotics in Division 2. You must complete the full raid on any difficulty level to have a chance at the drop. The drop operates on a weekly lockout per character, so running multiple characters through the raid each week maximizes your farming efficiency. Assemble a coordinated raid team for the best results.

Geri and Freki, Explained

Fire from one shoulder to load offensive primers, from the other to stack defensive ones — then detonate the pile for pure destruction. A double-barrel that demands a strategy session.

Build Tips

The Ravenous excels in burst DPS builds that capitalize on the prime-detonate combo. Negotiator's Dilemma gear set lets your critical hits chain to nearby enemies, amplifying the detonation's area impact. Pair with the Sharpshooter specialization for headshot damage bonuses and the digital scope. Coyote's Mask and Fox's Prayer knee pads provide multiplicative damage bonuses that boost both priming and detonation damage. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Geri and Freki work?
One shoulder loads offensive primers, the other stacks defensive ones; detonating primers unleashes the blast.
Where does The Ravenous drop?
Exclusively from Colonel Morozova in Operation Iron Horse, around ten percent.
