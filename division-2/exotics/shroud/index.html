How to Get Shroud in Division 2 [2026]

The Shroud exotic MMR guarantees a critical hit on your first scoped shot, making it a deadly choice for one-shot sniper builds. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Shroud at a Glance

TypeExotic MMR
TalentFirst Shot
SourceTargeted Loot (MMR), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

The Shroud drops randomly from targeted loot MMR areas and exotic caches. There is no quest chain required to unlock this weapon. Farm missions with MMR targeted loot on Heroic or Legendary difficulty for the best chances. Exotic Caches from weekly projects and season rewards are another reliable source.

First Shot, Explained

First Shot guarantees that the first bullet you fire after scoping in is a critical hit and deals amplified bonus damage. After you take the shot, you need to re-scope to reactivate the talent. This creates a deliberate, rhythmic playstyle where each carefully aimed shot delivers maximum impact. The guaranteed crit eliminates randomness and lets you build around consistent high-damage hits.

Build Tips

Shroud is ideal for one-shot builds that prioritize single, devastating headshots. Pair it with the Headhunter talent for stacking headshot damage that compounds with First Shot's guaranteed crit. Stack critical hit damage and headshot damage on your gear to amplify each shot. The Sharpshooter specialization provides stability and bipod support for steady aim. FAQ

How does the First Shot talent work on Shroud?
First Shot guarantees that your first shot after aiming is a critical hit and deals bonus damage. After firing, you must re-scope to activate the talent again.
Does Shroud work with Headhunter?
Yes, Shroud pairs extremely well with the Headhunter talent. The guaranteed crit from First Shot synergizes with Headhunter's stacking headshot damage for devastating one-shot potential.
Where can I farm the Shroud MMR?
Shroud drops from targeted loot MMR areas and exotic caches. There is no quest chain required — it is a random exotic drop from any eligible source.
