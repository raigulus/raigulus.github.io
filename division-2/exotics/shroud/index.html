How to Get Shroud in Division 2 [2026]

The Shroud exotic MMR guarantees a critical hit on your first scoped shot, making it a deadly choice for one-shot sniper builds. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

TypeExotic MMR

How to Unlock It

The Shroud drops randomly from targeted loot MMR areas and exotic caches. There is no quest chain required to unlock this weapon. Farm missions with MMR targeted loot on Heroic or Legendary difficulty for the best chances. Exotic Caches from weekly projects and season rewards are another reliable source.

High Priority Target, Explained

Deal +125% weapon damage to the highest-ranking enemies in the fight — the Overdogs mirror image. Aim at the heavies, ignore the chaff.

Build Tips

Shroud is ideal for priority-target builds that delete heavies first. Pair it with the Headhunter talent for stacking headshot damage that compounds with the tier amp. Stack critical hit damage and headshot damage on your gear to amplify each shot. The Sharpshooter specialization provides stability and bipod support for steady aim.
