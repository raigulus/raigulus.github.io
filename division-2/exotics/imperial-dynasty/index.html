The Division 2 / Exotic Holster

How to Get Imperial Dynasty in The Division 2

Imperial Dynasty is a fire-based exotic holster that automatically burns nearby enemies, making it essential for crowd control builds. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Imperial Dynasty at a Glance

TypeExotic Holster
TalentDragon's Glare
SourceCleaner named boss drops; Targeted Loot (Holster)

How to Unlock It

Farm Cleaner named bosses in the open world, especially in the New York expansion areas. You can also target Holsters as Targeted Loot.

Dragon's Glare, Explained

While in combat, the nearest enemy within twenty meters catches fire on a thirty-five-second loop. A pocket arsonist for status builds — pair it with burn talents and let proximity do the work.

Build Tips

Essential for fire and crowd control builds, especially with the Firewall specialization. Pair with Eclipse Protocol or Burn Gear Set for maximum effect.

FAQ

How does Dragon's Glare work?
In combat it burns the closest enemy within 20m every 35 seconds.
Where does Imperial Dynasty drop?
Cleaner bosses drop it most often; otherwise targeted holster loot and caches.
