How to Get Doctor Home in The Division 2

The Doctor Home is an exotic rifle that repairs your armor whenever you land a headshot kill, providing incredible sustain and survivability in all content. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Doctor Home at a Glance

TypeExotic Rifle
TalentTreatment
SourceGeneral Anderson Manhunt reward, Targeted Loot (Rifle)

How to Unlock It

Doctor Home is obtained as a reward for completing the General Anderson Manhunt. Complete all manhunt targets to unlock the final bounty against General Anderson, then defeat him to receive the Doctor Home. After obtaining it once, the rifle can also drop from targeted rifle activities and exotic caches throughout the game, giving you chances for additional drops or God-roll versions.

Treatment, Explained

Treatment triggers whenever you land a headshot kill, repairing 25% of your total armor instantly. Against groups of enemies, chaining headshot kills creates a continuous healing loop that keeps your armor at maximum without needing healing skills or armor kits. This makes Doctor Home particularly powerful in content with dense enemy spawns where you can maintain the healing chain. The talent rewards precision aiming and target prioritization, as body shot kills don't trigger the repair.

Build Tips

Doctor Home excels in headshot-focused builds that combine DPS with survivability. The Sharpshooter specialization provides headshot damage bonuses and the digital scope for improved accuracy. Equip Providence Defense for headshot damage and Weapon Handling, and Airaldi Holdings for bonus marksman rifle damage. For solo heroic and legendary content, Doctor Home lets you run DPS builds without sacrificing healing skills, opening up an extra skill slot for offensive abilities. FAQ

How much armor does Treatment repair on headshot kills?
Treatment repairs 25% of your total armor on every headshot kill. With a full 1.7 million armor build, that's over 425,000 armor repaired per kill. Against groups of enemies, chain headshot kills can keep you at full armor indefinitely, making you nearly unkillable.
Is Doctor Home good for solo heroic content?
Doctor Home is exceptional for solo heroic content because the Treatment talent provides constant self-healing. Headshot kills keep your armor topped off, letting you sustain through enemy fire without relying on healing skills or armor kits. Pair it with a DPS build for maximum efficiency.
Can Doctor Home drop from targeted loot?
Yes, Doctor Home can drop from targeted rifle activities and exotic caches after being obtained through the General Anderson Manhunt. Farm missions and open world activities with rifle targeted loot for additional chances beyond the manhunt reward.
