How to Get Tardigrade Armor System in Division 2 [2026]

The Tardigrade Armor System is a powerful exotic chest piece that shields your allies when they take heavy damage. Its Ablative Nano-Plating talent makes it one of the best support exotics in The Division 2. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Tardigrade Armor System at a Glance

TypeExotic Chest
TalentAblative Nano-Plating
SourceTrue Sons named boss drops; Targeted Loot (Chest)

How to Unlock It

The Tardigrade Armor System drops from True Sons named bosses throughout the open world and inside True Sons-controlled missions. Farming the Federal Triangle or Downtown East areas with Targeted Loot set to Chest pieces increases your odds. You can also earn it from Exotic Caches obtained through weekly projects, Summit challenges, or seasonal reward tracks.

Ablative Nano-Plating, Explained

When any ally in your group has their armor broken, Ablative Nano-Plating immediately grants them 80% bonus armor for 10 seconds. This effect has a 25-second cooldown per ally, meaning each teammate triggers the protection independently. The bonus armor scales with your own Armor stat, so stacking more armor on your build strengthens the shield you provide to teammates.

Build Tips

Tardigrade Armor System shines in group-oriented support and tank builds. Pair it with gear sets like Foundry Bulwark or Gila Guard to maximize your Armor value and strengthen the bonus armor granted to allies. It works exceptionally well alongside the Vanguard talent on your backpack, which grants bonus armor to allies when you use a shield. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

What does Tardigrade Armor System do in Division 2?
Tardigrade Armor System grants 80% bonus armor to an ally for 10 seconds whenever their armor breaks, with a 25-second cooldown per ally. It excels at keeping your team alive during tough encounters.
Where does Tardigrade Armor System drop in Division 2?
Tardigrade Armor System drops from True Sons named bosses in the open world and missions. You can also get it from Targeted Loot when Chest is the targeted slot, or from Exotic Caches.
Is Tardigrade Armor System good for solo play in Division 2?
Tardigrade Armor System is primarily a group-oriented exotic since its talent triggers on allies breaking armor. For solo play other chest pieces like Ridgeway's Pride or Providence may offer better personal damage or survivability.
