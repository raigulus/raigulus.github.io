How to Get Acosta's Go-Bag in The Division 2

Acosta's Go-Bag is a skill-focused exotic backpack that grants Overcharge when you throw a grenade. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Acosta's Go-Bag at a Glance

TypeExotic Backpack
TalentOne in Hand / Two in the Bag
SourceLocked faction box drops (random), Targeted Loot (Backpack)

How to Unlock It

Acosta's Go-Bag drops from locked faction boxes scattered throughout the open world. These require lockpicks or keys to open and have a random chance to contain the bag. You can also find it through targeted loot when backpacks are the active slot.

One in Hand / Two in the Bag, Explained

Throwing a grenade refunds it and grants +1 skill tier for fifteen seconds — overcharge if you are already at six. The bag itself carries extra armor kits, grenades, ammo, repair and status power.

Build Tips

Acosta's Go-Bag is a staple for skill builds that want periodic burst power. Time your grenades to Overcharge during tough encounters or boss fights. Pair with skills that benefit most from Overcharge like the Hive or Chem Launcher. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How do the Acosta talents work?
One in Hand refunds thrown grenades with +1 tier; Two in the Bag adds kits, grenades, ammo, repair and status.
Where does Acosta’s Go-Bag drop?
The Warlords-era backpack is in targeted backpack loot, faction caches and exotic caches.
