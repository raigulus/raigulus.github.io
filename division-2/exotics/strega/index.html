How to Get Strega in The Division 2

Strega is an exotic assault rifle that rewards aggressive play with stacking damage bonuses through the Rage talent, reaching up to 50% bonus damage at maximum stacks. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Strega at a Glance

TypeExotic Assault Rifle
TalentUnnerve
SourceTargeted Loot (AR), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Strega drops from any activity that awards exotic items, with targeted assault rifle loot sources providing the best farming efficiency. Run Countdown with AR targeting, clear Summit floors with assault rifle directives, or open Exotic Caches from weekly projects. The drop is RNG-based, so consistent farming across multiple activities improves your odds over time.

Unnerve, Explained

Every kill marks everything within twenty meters, and each mark means +15% amplified damage — stackable, refreshable, relentless. Keep killing, keep marking.

Build Tips

Strega excels in aggressive DPS builds that prioritize kill speed to build and maintain Rage stacks. Striker's Battlegear provides rate of fire bonuses that help chain kills faster, while Heartbreaker's bonus armor gives you room to trade hits without losing stacks. Consider run-and-gun playstyles where you keep moving to avoid taking damage while mowing down targets. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.
