How to Get The Chatterbox in The Division 2

The Chatterbox is an exotic SMG crafted from Hyena faction parts that boosts your rate of fire when reloading near enemies through the In Rhythm talent. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The Chatterbox at a Glance

TypeExotic SMG
TalentIn Rhythm
SourceCrafted from 3 Hyena parts + Blueprint from Bank HQ

How to Unlock It

The Chatterbox requires collecting three specific parts from Hyena faction caches and a blueprint from the Bank HQ mission. Part 1 drops from a Hyena cache in the bank vault underground area. Part 2 comes from Jefferson Trade Center. Part 3 is found in the Grand Washington Hotel. Once you have all three parts and the blueprint, craft it at any crafting bench.

In Rhythm, Explained

In Rhythm rewards you for reloading near enemies by increasing your rate of fire by 20% for 10 seconds. This effect stacks up to 3 times, meaning a fully stacked reload grants +60% RPM for 10 seconds. The talent encourages aggressive close-quarters play where you constantly push toward enemies, reload in their faces, and then melt them with the increased fire rate.

Build Tips

The Chatterbox is built for close-range DPS builds that can stay near enemies to maintain In Rhythm stacks. Hunter's Fury provides the disorient and bonus damage needed to survive in close quarters while the RPM boost shreds targets. Consider using a shield or Firewall specialization to safely close distance before triggering the talent. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Where do I find the three Chatterbox parts?
Part 1: Hyena cache in the bank vault underground area. Part 2: Jefferson Trade Center mission. Part 3: Grand Washington Hotel mission. All parts drop from Hyena faction caches.
Do I need the blueprint to craft Chatterbox?
Yes, after collecting all 3 parts, you get the blueprint from the Bank HQ mission. Take the parts and blueprint to a crafting bench to assemble The Chatterbox.
How many stacks can In Rhythm reach?
In Rhythm stacks up to 3 times, providing a total +60% RPM increase when fully stacked. Each stack lasts 10 seconds and refreshes when you reload near enemies.
