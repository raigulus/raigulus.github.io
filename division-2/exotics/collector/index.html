How to Get Collector in Division 2 [2026]

Collector is an exotic chest piece that increases your loot drops, making it the ultimate farming accessory in The Division 2. Its Hoarder talent gives every kill a chance at bonus items. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Collector at a Glance

TypeExotic Chest
TalentHoarder
SourceExotic Cache (random)

How to Unlock It

Collector is obtained randomly from Exotic Caches, which are the only source for this chest piece. You earn Exotic Caches by completing weekly SHD level projects, reaching milestones on the season pass, clearing Summit floor 100, defeating the Nemesis in Descent, and from certain manhunt rewards. Since the drop is random, expect to open multiple Exotic Caches before Collector appears.

Hoarder, Explained

Hoarder gives every kill you make a 10% chance to drop an additional loot item on top of the normal drop. The bonus item can be any rarity, including high-end, gear set, or even exotic items. This effect has no internal cooldown, so rapid kills in missions or the open world can trigger multiple extra drops in quick succession, significantly boosting your loot per hour.

Build Tips

Collector is designed for loot farming builds rather than combat optimization. Pair it with gear that boosts kill speed like Negotiator's Dilemma or Striker to maximize the number of kills and therefore the number of bonus loot rolls. Use it when running Targeted Loot missions or farming open-world bosses to accelerate your gear acquisition. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does the Hoarder talent work on Collector?
Hoarder gives each kill a 10% chance to drop an extra loot item. This additional drop can be any rarity including high-end or exotic items, making Collector a top choice for agents focused on farming gear and maximizing loot efficiency.
Where does Collector drop in Division 2?
Collector is obtained randomly from Exotic Caches. You earn Exotic Caches through weekly SHD level projects, season pass rewards, Summit floor 100 completions, and Descent Nemesis kills. There is no targeted farming method since it only comes from Exotic Caches.
Is Collector worth using in Division 2 endgame?
Collector is primarily a loot farming tool rather than a combat-focused exotic. Its Hoarder talent increases your loot intake over time, helping you acquire god-roll items faster. For pure combat performance, other chest pieces like Tardigrade or Ridgeway's Pride offer more direct combat benefits.
