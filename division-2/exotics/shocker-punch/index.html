The Division 2 / Exotic Holster

How to Get Shocker Punch in The Division 2

Shocker Punch is an exotic holster that adds a shock effect to your melee attacks, providing crowd control utility. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Shocker Punch at a Glance

TypeExotic Holster
TalentDefibrillator
SourceTargeted Loot (Holster); Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Farm Targeted Loot for Holsters in any mission or open world zone. They also drop from Exotic Caches earned through weekly activities.

Defibrillator, Explained

Shrugs off electric stuns faster and turns shields and melee into shock delivery — paired with the St. Elmo’s Engine it hits even harder on a short cooldown.

Build Tips

Useful for melee and close-quarters crowd control builds. Pair with a shield build for safe melee engagement in Heroic content.

FAQ

How does Defibrillator work?
Stun duration drops, shield use boosts melee, and pairing with St. Elmo’s adds shock blasts on a fifteen-second cooldown.
Where does Shocker Punch drop?
The Season 1 Year 5 holster is available from targeted holster loot and exotic caches.
