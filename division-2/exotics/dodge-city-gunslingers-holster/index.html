How to Get Dodge City Gunslinger's Holster in The Division 2

Dodge City Gunslinger's Holster is a pistol-focused exotic that delivers devastating one-shot damage on your first shot after swapping. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Dodge City Gunslinger's Holster at a Glance

TypeExotic Holster
TalentQuick Draw
SourceExotic quest with Blueprint from Inaya al-Khatoa

How to Unlock It

Visit Inaya al-Khatoa at the White House to unlock the exotic blueprint. Complete the crafting quest by farming the required materials, then craft the holster at any crafting station.

Quick Draw, Explained

While your pistol stays holstered it banks a stack every fraction of a second up to one hundred; the first shot after swapping spends them for +10% damage per stack — landing as headshot damage wherever it hits.

Build Tips

Best used in pistol one-shot builds or combo setups with the Regulus exotic revolver. Pair with Headhunter talent for maximum single-shot damage.

FAQ

How does Quick Draw work?
