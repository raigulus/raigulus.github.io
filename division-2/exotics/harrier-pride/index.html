How to Get Harrier Pride in The Division 2

Harrier Pride is a support exotic backpack that boosts nearby allies' weapon damage. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Harrier Pride at a Glance

TypeExotic Backpack
TalentRebalance
Source10th Anniversary Event; Targeted Loot (Backpack)

How to Unlock It

Harrier Pride was originally available during the 10th Anniversary Event. It can now drop from targeted loot when backpacks are the active slot and from exotic caches. Farm backpack targeted loot areas or wait for the event to return for another chance.

Rebalance, Explained

Hitting enemies builds red stacks for weapon damage, getting hit builds blue stacks for resistance — at eighty total the colors trade bonuses. Deal pain, take pain, come out balanced.

Build Tips

Harrier Pride shines in group DPS compositions where you want to boost everyone's damage. Pair it with a support-oriented loadout that keeps you alive and near teammates. It works well in raid and incursion settings where groups stay grouped. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Rebalance work?
Red stacks from dealing damage, blue from taking it; at 80 total they swap bonuses.
Where does Harrier Pride drop?
The anniversary backpack came from the event pass and caches, now in targeted loot.
