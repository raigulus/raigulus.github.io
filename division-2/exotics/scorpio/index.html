How to Get Scorpio in Division 2 [2026]

TypeExotic Shotgun

How to Unlock It

The Scorpio has no quest chain — it drops as a random exotic from any enemy or cache when Shotguns are the targeted loot category. Farm shotgun targeted loot zones on Heroic or Legendary difficulty for the best drop rates. Exotic Caches from weekly projects and season rewards also have a chance to contain Scorpio. Consistent farming is the key to obtaining this weapon.

Venomous, Explained

Venomous applies escalating status effects as you land hits on enemies. At 10 hits, the target is poisoned for 4 seconds, dealing damage over time. At 15 hits, they become disoriented for 6 seconds, impairing their aim and awareness. At 20 hits, the enemy is shocked for 3 seconds, completely disabling them. This stacking system makes Scorpio incredibly effective at controlling groups of enemies.

Build Tips

Scorpio shines in crowd control and status effect builds. Pair it with Eclipse Protocol for spreading status effects across groups, or use it with the Firewall specialization for close-range survivability. The Scorpio is also a strong pick for group play where your CC contributions amplify your team's damage output. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

What does the Venomous talent do on Scorpio?
Venomous applies escalating status effects based on hits: 10 hits applies poison for 4 seconds, 15 hits applies disorient for 6 seconds, and 20 hits applies shock for 3 seconds.
Where can I farm Scorpio in Division 2?
Scorpio drops from targeted loot shotgun areas, exotic caches, and has a chance to drop from any enemy when shotguns are the targeted loot category.
Is Scorpio good for group play?
Yes, Scorpio is excellent for group play because its stacking status effects provide consistent crowd control while dealing solid damage, making it a top pick for group CC builds.
