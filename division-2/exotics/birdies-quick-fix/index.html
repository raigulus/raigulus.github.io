How to Get Birdie's Quick Fix in The Division 2

Birdie's Quick Fix is a survival-oriented exotic backpack that auto-repairs armor when you take heavy damage. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Birdie's Quick Fix at a Glance

TypeExotic Backpack
TalentEmergency Button
SourceY6S3 Manhunt Climax guaranteed first Master clear

How to Unlock It

Birdie's Quick Fix is guaranteed on your first Master difficulty clear of the Y6S3 Manhunt Climax mission. Complete the full manhunt chain to unlock the Climax, then run it on Master. You will receive the backpack after defeating the final boss.

Emergency Button, Explained

Emergency Button automatically triggers when your armor drops below 30%. It instantly repairs 50% of your maximum armor, giving you a second chance in dangerous situations. The effect has a 60-second cooldown, so timing and positioning still matter between procs.

Build Tips

Birdie's Quick Fix is ideal for survival builds and solo play where you need a safety net. It pairs well with armor-stacking loadouts that benefit from the large heal. Use it when pushing aggressive content where burst damage is a constant threat. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How do you get Birdie's Quick Fix?
Birdie's Quick Fix is guaranteed from the Y6S3 Manhunt Climax on your first Master difficulty clear. It cannot be obtained from other sources.
How long is the Emergency Button cooldown?
Emergency Button has a 60-second cooldown. It triggers automatically when your armor drops below 30%, instantly repairing 50% of your armor.
Is Birdie's Quick Fix good for solo play?
Yes, Birdie's Quick Fix is excellent for solo survival builds. The automatic armor repair provides a safety net without needing to use skills or medkits.
