How to Get Pakhan in The Division 2

The Pakhan is an exotic LMG that rewards aggressive play with stacking damage buffs on every kill, turning you into a unstoppable force in prolonged fights. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Pakhan at a Glance

TypeExotic LMG
TalentWarlord
SourceTargeted Loot (LMG), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Pakhan drops from targeted LMG activities across the game, including missions, control points, and open world events. Exotic Caches from weekly SHD level projects and the season pass provide another path to obtaining it. Farming the Capitol Building or Lincoln Memorial missions with LMG targeted loot active gives efficient drop chances due to the number ofNamed enemies.

Warlord, Explained

Each kill with Pakhan grants one stack of Warlord, up to a maximum of 5 stacks. Every stack adds 8% weapon damage, meaning at full stacks you deal 40% more damage. The stacks persist as long as you keep firing, but begin to decay after a few seconds of inactivity. This creates a rewarding gameplay loop where chain-killing enemies maintains your damage at peak levels, making Pakhan devastating in missions with tightly packed enemy spawns.

Build Tips

Pakhan pairs exceptionally well with sustained DPS builds. The Striker gear set's stacking rate of fire and damage bonuses compound with Warlord's stacks for exponential damage growth. The Heartbreaker set's bonus armor and weapon damage also synergize beautifully. Use the Gunner specialization for ammo regeneration and Banshee Pulse to keep enemies close while you maintain your fire. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How many stacks can Pakhan's Warlord talent reach?
Pakhan's Warlord talent can stack up to 5 times, with each stack granting 8% weapon damage. At maximum stacks you gain 40% bonus weapon damage, which persists until you stop firing for a few seconds. Maintaining fire across multiple kills keeps the stacks active.
Is Pakhan good for solo play?
Pakhan is excellent for solo play since you control the pace of engagements. The stacking damage buff rewards aggressive playstyle and sustained fire, making it ideal for clearing control points and missions where you can chain kills without downtime.
What builds work best with Pakhan?
Pakhan pairs well with DPS builds using Striker or Heartbreaker gear sets. The stacking damage buff compounds with rate of fire bonuses, and the Gunner specialization's ammo regen keeps you shooting through prolonged fights. Critical hit builds with Coyote's Mask also work well.
