How to Get Big Alejandro in The Division 2

The Big Alejandro is an exotic LMG that rewards hip-firing with massively increased rate of fire, turning it into a close-range shredder. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Big Alejandro at a Glance

TypeExotic LMG
TalentBreadbasket
Source10th Anniversary Event, Targeted Loot (LMG)

How to Unlock It

Big Alejandro was initially introduced as a reward during the Division 2's 10th Anniversary Event. It can now drop from targeted LMG activities and exotic caches throughout the game. Farm missions and control points with LMG targeted loot, or complete weekly SHD projects that reward exotic caches. The weapon's unique hip-fire mechanic makes it a sought-after exotic for players who enjoy aggressive close-quarters combat.

Breadbasket, Explained

When you hip-fire Big Alejandro, the Breadbasket talent activates and begins stacking rate of fire bonuses. Each stack adds 20% RPM, stacking up to 3 times for a total of 60% bonus rate of fire. At maximum stacks, Big Alejandro fires at an absurd rate that shreds enemies at close range. The stacks reset if you aim down sights, so the weapon rewards a run-and-gun playstyle where you stay mobile and keep firing from the hip.

Build Tips

Big Alejandro is built for aggressive close-range builds. The Hunter's Fury gear set rewards close-range kills with disorient and damage bonuses, perfectly complementing the hip-fire playstyle. The Gunner specialization provides ammo regeneration and Banshee Pulse for crowd control. Equip pieces with critical hit chance and weapon damage to maximize the shredding potential at maximum RPM stacks. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How much bonus RPM does Breadbasket give?
Breadbasket grants 20% bonus rate of fire while hip-firing, stacking up to 3 times for a total of 60% bonus RPM. This transforms Big Alejandro into a bullet hose that melts enemies at close range. The stacks reset if you aim down sights.
Is Big Alejandro good in PvP?
Big Alejandro can be strong in Conflict PvP and Dark Zone skirmishes where close-range combat is common. The high RPM from hip-fire makes it deadly in tight spaces, though you'll need to master movement and positioning to land shots without aiming.
