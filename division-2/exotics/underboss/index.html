How to Get Underboss in The Division 2

Underboss is an exotic SMG with the Gangland Hit talent that marks enemies for amplified damage, making it a top-tier choice for group play. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Underboss at a Glance

TypeExotic SMG
TalentGangland Hit
SourceZodiac Climax Mission (guaranteed first Master clear)

How to Unlock It

Underboss is guaranteed to drop on your first Master difficulty clear of the Zodiac Climax mission. After obtaining it from the mission, it can also drop from targeted SMG loot sources in Countdown, Summit, or Exotic Caches. The guaranteed first clear means every agent has a reliable path to obtaining this exotic.

Gangland Hit, Explained

Gangland Hit marks enemies you damage, causing them to take +20% amplified damage from all sources. When playing in a group, each ally who also marks the same target adds an additional +5% amplified damage per ally. In a full 4-player group, a single marked target can receive up to +35% amplified damage, making Underboss one of the strongest group utility weapons in the game.

Build Tips

Underboss shines brightest in group play where Gangland Hit's stacking amplified damage benefits the entire team. Pair it with DPS builds that prioritize mark application and target switching. The amplified damage works well with any build that focuses on single-target elimination. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Do I need to clear Zodiac Climax on Master difficulty?
Yes, Underboss is guaranteed on your first Master difficulty clear of the Zodiac Climax mission. After that first clear, it can drop from targeted SMG loot sources.
How does Gangland Hit work in groups?
Each ally who marks the same target adds an extra +5% amplified damage. In a full 4-player group, a marked target can receive up to +35% amplified damage from Gangland Hit alone.
Is Underboss viable for solo play?
Yes, the base +20% amplified damage on marked targets is strong for solo play. The group bonus is a nice addition but not required for the weapon to be effective.
