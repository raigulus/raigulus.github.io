How to Get Sweet Dreams in Division 2 [2026]

Sweet Dreams is a brutal exotic shotgun that lets you execute weakened enemies with a single melee strike. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sweet Dreams at a Glance

TypeExotic Shotgun
TalentSandman
SourceOutcast Named Boss Drops, Targeted Loot (Shotgun)

How to Unlock It

Sweet Dreams drops primarily from Outcast named bosses found throughout the game. Farm Outcast-controlled missions and Control Points on Heroic difficulty to maximize your chances. Targeted loot shotgun zones also have a chance to drop it, along with exotic caches from weekly projects. Keep an eye out for named Outcast enemies during control point assaults.

Sandman, Explained

The Sandman talent instantly kills any enemy below 30% HP when you perform a melee attack on them. This execution ability has a 9-second cooldown between uses, so timing your melee strikes is critical. The talent makes Sweet Dreams devastating in close-quarters combat, allowing you to finish off wounded targets without wasting ammunition.

Build Tips

Sweet Dreams thrives in aggressive close-range builds. Pair it with the Firewall specialization for maximum melee effectiveness and survivability at close range. Shield builds work extremely well since you can close distance safely before delivering the Sandman finish. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does the Sandman talent work on Sweet Dreams?
Sandman instantly kills any enemy below 30% HP when you melee them. It has a 9-second cooldown between uses, making it a powerful execution tool in close-range combat.
What is the best way to farm Sweet Dreams?
Sweet Dreams drops from Outcast named bosses, primarily in Outcast-controlled missions and areas. You can also get it from targeted loot shotgun zones and exotic caches.
Which specialization works best with Sweet Dreams?
The Firewall specialization is ideal for Sweet Dreams because it grants a flamethrower for close-range encounters and skills that synergize with aggressive melee-focused playstyles.
