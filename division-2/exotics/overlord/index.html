How to Get Overlord in Division 2 [2026]

The Overlord exotic shotgun deals amplified damage to bleeding enemies, making it a powerhouse in status effect builds. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Overlord at a Glance

TypeExotic Shotgun
TalentCrushing
SourceTargeted Loot (Shotgun), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Overlord drops randomly from targeted loot shotgun areas and exotic caches. There is no specific quest or guaranteed drop route for this weapon. Farm shotgun targeted loot zones on Heroic or Legendary difficulty, and open every exotic cache you can from weekly projects and season rewards. The drop rates are standard for exotic shotguns.

Crushing, Explained

The Crushing talent amplifies all damage you deal with the Overlord to enemies currently affected by bleed. When an enemy is bleeding from any source — your skills, gear talents, or weapon effects — your shotgun shots against them deal significantly increased damage. This creates a powerful synergy loop where bleed application directly multiplies your damage output.

Build Tips

Overlord is the centerpiece of bleed-focused builds. Pair it with Eclipse Protocol to spread bleed across groups, then watch Crushing amplify your damage on every bleeding target. Skills like Bleeding MIME or the Firestarter chem launcher that apply consistent bleed make Overlord shine. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does the Crushing talent work on Overlord?
Crushing amplifies damage when shooting enemies that are affected by bleed. The more bleed damage the target is taking, the more bonus damage your shotgun shots deal.
What builds work best with Overlord?
Overlord excels in bleed-focused builds using Eclipse Protocol, Bleeding MIME, or skills that apply bleed. The synergy between consistent bleed application and Crushing amplification creates massive damage output.
Where can I find Overlord in Division 2?
Overlord drops from targeted loot shotgun areas and exotic caches. Farm shotgun zones on Heroic or Legendary difficulty for the best chances at obtaining this exotic.
