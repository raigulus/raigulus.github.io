How to Get Overlord in Division 2 [2026]

The Overlord exotic shotgun deals amplified damage to bleeding enemies, making it a powerhouse in status effect builds. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

How to Unlock It

Overlord drops randomly from targeted loot shotgun areas and exotic caches. There is no specific quest or guaranteed drop route for this weapon. Farm shotgun targeted loot zones on Heroic or Legendary difficulty, and open every exotic cache you can from weekly projects and season rewards. The drop rates are standard for exotic shotguns.

Capitulate, Explained

Every pellet that lands slows the target, stacking movement penalty for five seconds — a pump-action leash for runners.

Build Tips

Overlord is the centerpiece of crowd-control builds. Pair it with Eclipse Protocol to slow whole groups, then punish runners while Capitulate keeps them crawling. Status-spreading skills that keep enemies tagged make Overlord shine. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Capitulate work?
Hits apply -4% movement speed per pellet for 5s, refreshed on re-hit.
Where does Overlord drop?
First Rogue season track reward, then targeted shotgun loot and caches.
