How to Get The Bighorn in The Division 2

The Bighorn is a legendary exotic assault rifle that rewards precision aim with devastating headshot damage, especially when aiming down sights. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The Bighorn at a Glance

TypeExotic Assault Rifle
TalentHeadsplitter
SourceLegendary Strongholds (Roosevelt Island, Capitol Building, District Union Arena)

How to Unlock It

The Bighorn only drops from Legendary Stronghold completions. You need a well-coordinated team with optimized builds to tackle the increased enemy health and damage on Legendary difficulty. Each of the three Strongholds has an equal chance to drop it. Run them weekly for the best odds, and consider using multiple characters to increase your attempts.

Headsplitter, Explained

Headsplitter provides a baseline +30% headshot damage bonus at all times. When you aim down sights, this bonus jumps to a massive 200% headshot damage increase, but your weapon stability is severely reduced. The trade-off is clear: precise shots deal enormous damage, but spraying becomes nearly impossible. This makes The Bighorn a skill-based weapon that rewards patient, accurate shooting over trigger discipline.

Build Tips

The Bighorn pairs naturally with the Sharpshooter specialization for additional headshot damage and stability compensation. Headshot-focused builds with Providence Defense orAiraldi Holdings gear sets amplify the Headsplitter bonus to devastating levels. Consider using a stability mod or weapon handling attributes to offset the ADS stability penalty. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

Which Legendary Stronghold drops The Bighorn?
The Bighorn can drop from any of the three Legendary Strongholds: Roosevelt Island, Capitol Building, and District Union Arena. All three have equal drop rates.
What does Headsplitter do when aiming?
While aiming down sights, Headsplitter grants 200% headshot damage but drastically reduces weapon stability. You trade control for massive single-shot potential.
Is The Bighorn worth the Legendary grind?
If you enjoy precision headshot play, The Bighorn is one of the highest damage exotic ARs in the game. The stability penalty is manageable with practice and the Sharpshooter specialization.
