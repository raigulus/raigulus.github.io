How to Get The Bighorn in The Division 2

The Bighorn is a legendary exotic assault rifle that rewards precision aim with devastating headshot damage, especially when aiming down sights. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The Bighorn at a Glance

TypeExotic Assault Rifle
TalentBig Game Hunter
SourceLegendary Strongholds (Roosevelt Island, Capitol Building, District Union Arena)

How to Unlock It

The Bighorn only drops from Legendary Stronghold completions. You need a well-coordinated team with optimized builds to tackle the increased enemy health and damage on Legendary difficulty. Each of the three Strongholds has an equal chance to drop it. Run them weekly for the best odds, and consider using multiple characters to increase your attempts.

Big Game Hunter, Explained

Scoping switches to semi-automatic 450% shots while headshots stack bonus damage up to fifty with slow decay. Legendary strongholds and Summit only.

Build Tips

The Bighorn pairs naturally with the Sharpshooter specialization for additional headshot damage and stability compensation. Headshot-focused builds with Providence Defense orAiraldi Holdings gear sets amplify the Big Game Hunter stacks to devastating levels. Consider using a stability mod or weapon handling attributes to offset the ADS stability penalty. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Big Game Hunter work?
Scoped shots go semi-auto at 450% damage; headshots stack +2% headshot damage to fifty stacks.
Where does The Bighorn drop?
Legendary strongholds and Legendary Summit bosses at around five percent.
