How to Get Sacrum Imperium in Division 2 [2026]

Sacrum Imperium exotic MMR applies a damage-amplifying mark on kills, letting you chain through enemy groups with escalating lethality. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sacrum Imperium at a Glance

TypeExotic MMR
TalentMark of Death
SourceTargeted Loot (MMR), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Sacrum Imperium drops randomly from targeted loot MMR areas and exotic caches. There is no quest chain or specific mission required to obtain it. Farm missions with MMR targeted loot on Heroic or Legendary difficulty for the best drop rates. Exotic Caches from weekly projects and season rewards also have a chance to contain this weapon.

Mark of Death, Explained

Mark of Death applies a visible mark to any enemy you kill with the Sacrum Imperium. Marked targets take amplified damage from all sources — your weapons, skills, and your teammates' attacks. This creates a powerful chain-kill loop: kill one enemy, mark the next, and the mark makes them easier to eliminate. In group play, the mark benefits everyone, making Sacrum Imperium a valuable team asset.

Build Tips

Sacrum Imperium excels in chain-kill sniper builds. Prioritize accuracy and headshot damage to secure the initial kill quickly, then let Mark of Death handle the rest. In group play, coordinate with teammates to focus-fire marked targets for maximum efficiency. The talent synergizes well with damage-boosting skills and gear that amplify marked target damage further. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does the Mark of Death talent work?
Mark of Death applies a mark to enemies you kill. Marked targets take amplified damage from all sources. This creates a chain-kill loop where each kill makes the next target easier to eliminate.
Where does Sacrum Imperium drop?
Sacrum Imperium drops from targeted loot MMR areas and exotic caches. There is no quest chain required — it is a random exotic drop from any eligible source.
What makes Sacrum Imperium unique among MMRs?
Sacrum Imperium is the only MMR that applies debuffs to enemies through kills. The Mark of Death chain effect makes it exceptional for clearing groups of enemies in rapid succession.
