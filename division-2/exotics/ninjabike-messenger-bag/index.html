How to Get NinjaBike Messenger Bag in The Division 2

The NinjaBike Messenger Bag is a wildcard exotic backpack that counts as one piece of every equipped set. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

NinjaBike Messenger Bag at a Glance

TypeExotic Backpack
TalentFolderol
SourceTargeted Loot (Backpack), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

The NinjaBike Messenger Bag drops from targeted loot in the backpack slot or from exotic caches. Farm areas with backpack targeted loot active, and open every exotic cache you earn from weekly projects. There is no guaranteed mission source.

Folderol, Explained

Folderol makes the NinjaBike count as 1 piece of every brand set and gear set you have equipped. If you run two pieces of a brand, the NinjaBike counts as a third — activating the 3-piece bonus. This enables multi-brand and hybrid setups that would otherwise be impossible due to gear slot limitations.

Build Tips

The NinjaBike Messenger Bag is the backbone of creative hybrid builds. Mix two-piece brand bonuses from multiple brands to stack diverse stat boosts. It pairs well with any combination where you want more set bonuses than slots allow. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does the Folderol talent work?
Folderol acts as a wildcard — it counts as 1 piece of every brand and gear set you have equipped. This lets you activate multiple set bonuses with fewer pieces.
Where does NinjaBike Messenger Bag drop?
The NinjaBike Messenger Bag drops from targeted loot (Backpack) or exotic caches. There is no mission-specific drop location.
Is NinjaBike good for hybrid builds?
Yes, NinjaBike is the foundation of many hybrid builds. Its wildcard effect lets you mix brands and sets that would normally require too many slots.
