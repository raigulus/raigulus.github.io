How to Get NinjaBike Messenger Bag in The Division 2

The NinjaBike Messenger Bag is a wildcard exotic backpack that counts as one piece of every equipped set. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

TypeExotic Backpack

How to Unlock It

The NinjaBike Messenger Bag drops from targeted loot in the backpack slot or from exotic caches. Farm areas with backpack targeted loot active, and open every exotic cache you earn from weekly projects. There is no guaranteed mission source.

Resourceful, Explained

Counts toward any equipped gear-set or brand-set bonus, letting you mix set bonuses that normally demand full dedication. The Division 1 favorite, back.

Build Tips

The NinjaBike Messenger Bag is the backbone of creative hybrid builds. Mix two-piece brand bonuses from multiple brands to stack diverse stat boosts. It pairs well with any combination where you want more set bonuses than slots allow. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

What makes the NinjaBike backpack special?
Its talent counts toward any equipped gear or brand set bonus, breaking the usual set math.
Where does NinjaBike drop?
The Season 11 returning exotic is available from targeted backpack loot and exotic caches.
