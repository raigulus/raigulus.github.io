How to Get Caduceus in Division 2 [2026]

Caduceus is an exotic MK16 assault rifle that repairs you and your allies on critical hits. It turns a high-crit build into a walking support station without sacrificing damage. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Caduceus at a Glance

TypeExotic Assault Rifle (MK16)
TalentCaduceus - critical hits repair allies
SourceY8S2 Season Track; assault-rifle targeted loot as of Y8S3

How to Unlock It

Caduceus comes from the Year 8 Season 2 season track. As of Year 8 Season 3 it also appears in assault-rifle targeted loot - check today's targeted loot before you farm, and run the matching missions for the most rolls per hour.

Caduceus Talent, Explained

Critical hits repair you and your allies for 3% of the damage dealt. Every bullet that crits becomes team sustain, so critical-hit chance is the most important stat on the build, followed by critical-hit damage to scale both the hits and the heals. The repair has no cooldown, which rewards sustained fire on grouped enemies.

Build Tips

Caduceus runs at 650 RPM with a 30-round magazine and a fast 2.02s reload, so uptime is excellent. Stack critical-hit chance toward the cap, then critical-hit damage. The MK16 handles mid-range lanes well, letting you hold angles and keep the squad healed from cover. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.
