How to Get Diamondback in The Division 2

The Diamondback is an exotic rifle that randomly marks 5 enemies, granting guaranteed critical hits and bonus damage on shots against marked targets. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Diamondback at a Glance

TypeExotic Rifle
TalentAgonizing Bite
SourceKenly College Expedition first clear

How to Unlock It

Diamondback is obtained by completing the Kenly College Expedition for the first time. Kenly College is a rotating available content that cycles in and out of the game, so check your map to see if it's currently accessible. When Kenly College isn't available, exotic caches from weekly SHD projects and the season pass provide an alternative path to obtaining this exotic rifle.

Agonizing Bite, Explained

Mark a target and enjoy guaranteed critical hits for five seconds. Originally earned at Kenly College, reworked into a marking execution tool.

Build Tips

Diamondback shines in mark-and-burst DPS builds that capitalize on guaranteed crits. The Sharpshooter specialization's headshot damage bonuses pair perfectly since every marked shot crits. Equip the Airaldi Holdings set for bonus marksman damage and the Providence Defense set for critical hit damage. Since crits are guaranteed, you can focus other attributes on weapon damage and headshot damage instead of critical hit chance. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Agonizing Bite work?
Hitting a mark grants all shots guaranteed crits for five seconds after the mark lands.
Where does Diamondback drop?
Originally a Kenly College reward, now available from targeted rifle loot and exotic caches.
