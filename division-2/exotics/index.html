How to Farm Exotics

General pool: Most exotics drop from the general loot pool. Set the correct weapon type or gear slot as Targeted Loot in Countdown, The Summit, or the open world map to increase your odds.

Activity-locked: Raid and incursion exotics only drop from their specific activity on a weekly lockout per character. These cannot be target-farmed.

Quest/season-locked: Some exotics come from quest chains or season tracks and are guaranteed rewards rather than random drops.

FAQ

How many exotics are in Division 2 in 2026?
As of Y8S3 Red Horizon there are 78 exotics: 44 weapons and 34 armor pieces.
How do you farm exotics in Division 2?
Most exotics drop from the general loot pool and can be target-farmed by setting the correct weapon type or gear slot as Targeted Loot in Countdown, The Summit, or the open world map. Raid and incursion exotics are locked to weekly rolls.
What is the best exotic in Division 2?
It depends on your build. Popular picks include Coyote's Mask for group crit buffs, Memento for solo play, St. Elmo's Engine for shock utility, and Scorpio for crowd control.
Can you target-farm raid exotics?
No. Raid exotics like Eagle Bearer, The Ravenous, Regulus, and Ouroboros only drop from their specific activity on a weekly lockout per character.
