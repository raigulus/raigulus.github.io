FAQ

How do you get exotics in Division 2 Red Horizon?
Fafnir and Iron Will come from the free Season Pass track. Loaded for Bear is the Climax Mission exclusive reward at the end of the season.
Which Red Horizon exotic should you get first?
Fafnir and Iron Will arrive through normal season progression, so most players unlock them first. Loaded for Bear requires finishing the Climax Mission.
