How to Get Blacklisters in The Division 2

Blacklisters are exotic kneepads that reward kills with stacking movement speed for faster repositioning. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Blacklisters at a Glance

TypeExotic Kneepads
TalentUnderworld
SourceY7S2 Manhunt Climax guaranteed first Master clear

How to Unlock It

Complete the Year 7 Season 2 Manhunt Climax mission on Master difficulty for a guaranteed first drop. They can also drop from Targeted Loot (Kneepads).

Underworld, Explained

Underworld grants +10% movement speed for 10 seconds on each kill, stacking up to 3 times for a total of +30% movement speed. This makes you increasingly faster as you chain kills.

Build Tips

Great for mobile speed builds and rusher playstyles. Pair with an SMG or shotgun build for close-quarters combat where movement speed matters most.

FAQ

How many stacks can Underworld reach?
Underworld stacks up to 3 times, granting a maximum of +30% movement speed.
Do the stacks refresh on new kills?
Yes. Each new kill refreshes the duration of all current stacks for another 10 seconds.
What happens if I stop getting kills?
The stacks decay after 10 seconds without a kill. You lose all speed bonuses until you start killing again.
