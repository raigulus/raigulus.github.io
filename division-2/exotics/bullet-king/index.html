How to Get Bullet King in The Division 2

The Bullet King is an exotic LMG that never needs to reload, giving you unlimited sustained firepower against any enemy. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Bullet King at a Glance

TypeExotic LMG
TalentBullet Hell
SourceRikers named boss drops, Targeted Loot (LMG)

How to Unlock It

Bullet King drops from Rikers faction named bosses throughout the open world and in missions. You can farm it by running LMG targeted loot activities, focusing on Control Points and territory controls controlled by Rikers. The Capitol Building stronghold and Wall Street mission are particularly efficient farming spots due to their high density of named enemies.

Bullet Hell, Explained

Bullet Hell removes the reload mechanic entirely from Bullet King. Your magazine never depletes, meaning you can hold down the trigger indefinitely as long as you have reserve ammunition. The trade-off is a slightly lower base damage compared to other exotic LMGs, but the zero downtime more than compensates in prolonged engagements where every reload animation would cost you precious DPS.

Build Tips

Bullet King excels in sustained DPS builds where consistent damage output matters more than burst. The Striker gear set amplifies your rate of fire and stacking damage, creating a lethal combination with zero reload downtime. Pair it with the Gunner specialization for ammo regeneration and rate of fire buffs on kills. The Coyote's Mask and Ceska Vyroba pieces boost critical hit chance for even more consistent damage. FAQ

Does Bullet King have infinite ammo?
Bullet King doesn't have infinite ammo, but it never needs to reload. Your magazine never empties, so you can fire continuously as long as you have reserve ammunition. This makes it ideal for sustained DPS situations where reload downtime would normally cost you damage.
What is the best specialization for Bullet King?
The Gunner specialization is ideal for Bullet King. The Gunner's rate of fire buff on kills stacks perfectly with sustained fire, and the ammo regeneration passive ensures you never run dry. The Banshee Pulse also provides crowd control while you keep shooting.
Can Bullet King drop from Dark Zone?
Yes, Bullet King can drop from Rikers named boss encounters and targeted LMG loot in both PvE and Dark Zone activities. Farming Rikers Control Points and missions with LMG targeted loot gives you the best odds.
