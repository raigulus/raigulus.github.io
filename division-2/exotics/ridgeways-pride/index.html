How to Get Ridgeway's Pride in Division 2 [2026]

Ridgeway's Pride is an exotic chest piece that applies bleed on every shot, making it a cornerstone for damage-over-time builds in The Division 2. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Ridgeway's Pride at a Glance

TypeExotic Chest
TalentBleeding Edge
SourceSummit quest (5 parts on floors 1-100)

How to Unlock It

Ridgeway's Pride is obtained by completing a five-part quest inside the Summit. You need to collect scattered components at specific floor ranges from floor 1 up to floor 100. Each quest step requires reaching a designated floor tier and locating an item, so expect several Summit runs before assembling the full chest piece. Once all five parts are collected, the blueprint unlocks and you can craft Ridgeway's Pride at the crafting station.

Bleeding Edge, Explained

Shooting enemies within fifteen meters applies bleed, and every bleeding enemy nearby repairs a slice of your armor per second — from a trickle at one to nearly half at five. Bleed them all, outlast everything.

Build Tips

Ridgeway's Pride is the foundation of bleed and damage-over-time builds. Pair it with the Eclipse Protocol gear set to spread bleed to nearby enemies on kill, or use Ongoing Directive for bonus bleed damage. The Lady Death SMG synergizes perfectly since it applies its own bleed, and the Sadist weapon talent deals extra damage to bleeding targets. Equip Banshee Pulse or Chem Launcher Firestarter for crowd control. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How does Bleeding Edge work?
Hits within 15m apply bleed; each bleeding enemy nearby repairs 3-48% armor per second, scaling with their count.
How do you get Ridgeway’s Pride?
Clear all 100 Summit floors collecting five vest parts, then finish donation and hunter projects for the blueprint.
