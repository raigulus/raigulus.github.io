How to Get Overdogs in Division 2 [2026]

Overdogs are exotic gloves that reward kill streaks with massive weapon damage bonuses. They are a top pick for aggressive DPS agents in The Division 2. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Overdogs at a Glance

TypeExotic Gloves
TalentAlpha
SourceTargeted Loot (Gloves), Exotic Caches

How to Unlock It

Overdogs can drop from any source that awards exotic gloves in The Division 2. The best farming approach is setting Targeted Loot to Gloves and running missions or open-world activities in that zone. Countdown and Summit floor 100 are also efficient farming spots. Exotic Caches from weekly SHD level projects, season pass rewards, and Descent Nemesis kills can randomly award Overdogs as well.

Alpha, Explained

Alpha grants +5% weapon damage for 10 seconds each time you kill an enemy. This buff stacks up to 10 times, giving you a maximum of +50% weapon damage at full stacks. Each new kill refreshes the duration of all existing stacks. This encourages an aggressive playstyle where you push from target to target, building and maintaining your damage bonus through continuous eliminations.

Build Tips

Overdogs thrive on fast-paced DPS builds that chain kills together. Pair them with the Striker gear set for stacking weapon damage or Heartbreaker for armor-based sustained fire. Fast-firing weapons like the Chameleon SMG or Famas assault rifle help you build stacks quickly. The Gunner specialization providesammo regeneration to maintain your kill tempo.
