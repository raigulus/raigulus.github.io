How to Get Tinkerer in The Division 2

Tinkerer is an exotic mask that refunds skill charges on kills, enabling endless ability spam. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Tinkerer at a Glance

TypeExotic Mask
TalentDeconstruction
SourceY7S1 Manhunt Climax guaranteed first Master clear

How to Unlock It

Tinkerer is guaranteed on your first Master difficulty clear of the Y7S1 Manhunt Climax mission. Complete the full manhunt chain to unlock the Climax mission, then select Master difficulty. You will receive Tinkerer after defeating the final boss on your first completion.

Deconstruction, Explained

Deconstruction gives each skill kill a 20% chance to refund one skill charge. This effect works with any skill that has multiple charges, like the Sticky Bomb or Bombardier Drone. The refund triggers immediately on the kill, letting you chain abilities without waiting for cooldowns.

Build Tips

Tinkerer excels in skill spam builds running Tech or Skill Tier loadouts. Prioritize skills with charges to maximize the refund value. Pair with Hard Wired or Gear Sets that boost skill performance. For full setups see tested Division 2 builds.

FAQ

How do you get the Tinkerer mask?
Tinkerer is guaranteed from the Y7S1 Manhunt Climax on your first Master difficulty clear. It cannot be farmed from other sources.
Does Deconstruction work with all skills?
Yes, Deconstruction works with any skill that scores kills. Skills with multiple charges like the Sticky Bomb benefit most from the refund chance.
What is the best build for Tinkerer?
Tinkerer shines in skill spam builds running Tech or Skill Tier loadouts. Pair with skills that have charges to maximize refund value.
