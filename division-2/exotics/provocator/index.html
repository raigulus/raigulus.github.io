How to Get Provocator in Division 2 [2026]

Provocator is an exotic chest piece that rewards aggressive play by boosting your weapon damage after taking a hit. It is a strong choice for DPS-focused agents who thrive in close-quarters combat. Unofficial fan guide by Raigulus.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Provocator at a Glance

TypeExotic Chest
TalentChallenger
SourceY6S2 Space Admin Theo Parnell Manhunt Climax (first Master clear)

How to Unlock It

Provocator is guaranteed from completing the Y6S2 Space Admin Theo Parnell Manhunt Climax mission on Master difficulty for the first time. You must first complete all preceding manhunt targets to unlock the Climax mission node on the manhunt screen. Select Master difficulty before launching and clear the mission to receive Provocator as a guaranteed drop on your first completion.

Challenger, Explained

Nearby enemies make you tougher: damage resistance climbs while hostiles crowd within twenty meters. The brawler’s chest — walk into the mob, walk back out.

Build Tips

Provocator excels in aggressive DPS builds that push into enemy positions. Pair it with gear that provides armor on kill or bonus armor to sustain through the damage you take. The Striker gear set or Heartbreaker set pairs well since both reward sustained fire. Consider using Obligate or Flatline weapon talents to stack even more damage during the Challenger window. FAQ

How does Challenger work?
+25% damage resistance against enemies within 20m in PvE, updated higher in current tuning.

Shades of Red climax mission, guaranteed on first Master clear.
