The Division 2 / Y5S1 Update

Division 2 Broken Wings Update Guide

Year 5 Season 1 launched on June 8, 2023 with a twist nobody expected: the manhunt targets are civilians to rescue, not rogues to hunt. Plus a brand-new rogue-lite mode anyone can play. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Rescue Manhunt

Instead of chasing rogue agents, you pull hostages out of Black Tusk hands: John Yazzie, Eva Garcia, Lauren Fong and Mitchell Billings first, then ex-aquarium director Mari Singh from the zoo. Every rescue feeds the Castle settlement rebuild while Natalya Sokolova plays power politics in the background. Target order on our manhunts hub.

Descent Mode

A free 1-to-4 player rogue-lite with no expansion gate: you drop into a high-tech bunker simulation stripped of gear and SHD tech, then draft weapons, skills and talents floor by floor against an endlessly scaling curve. My solo run reaching Nemesis with nothing is on the channel — perfect first taste before the weekly push. Mode notes live on our Escalation hub.

A Splinter Cell outfit headlines the season pass, with SHD and Black Tusk vanity arriving in the mid-season apparel event.

FAQ

When did Broken Wings launch?
Year 5 Season 1 launched on June 8, 2023, opening with a rescue-style manhunt and the free Descent rogue-lite mode.
Do you need the expansion to play Descent?
No. Descent is free for all players and does not require Warlords of New York ownership, unlike the seasonal manhunt track.
