The Division 2 / Y6S2 Update

Division 2 Shades of Red Update Guide

Year 6 Season 2 launched on October 28, 2024 with the Seasons 2.0 model: a structured 7-mission Journey, four modifier types, Priority Objectives and weekly Manhunt Scouts chasing Theo Parnell. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Seasons 2.0

The seasonal model was rebuilt around guidance: a Journey of 7 sequential missions with 8 objectives each, modifiers in global, active, passive and hostile flavors (up to three passives at once), Priority Objectives offering blind-pick challenges with re-rolls, and a manhunt advancing through weekly scouts instead of monthly drops.

Story: Parnell

With Keener in custody, the season hunts his crew starting with Theo Parnell, while free-weekend events onboarded new agents into the Journey system.
