The Division 2 / Y8S3 Update

Division 2 Red Horizon Update Guide

Year 8 Season 3 launched on August 27, 2026. The Cleaners push into Washington DC under a new global modifier, three exotics join the loot pool, and the Determined talent gets its biggest rework in years. Below is our plain-language summary — numbers are facts from the official notes, sentences are ours. Full texts: Ubisoft's Red Horizon news hub.

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Season Theme

Cleaners bring fire to DC. The seasonal modifier fills a Pressure Gauge through kills and unlocks active modifiers that no longer share cooldowns. A first-ever non-normalized Dark Zone variant (Blackout) keeps SHD Watch and Expertise power intact.

New Exotics

Fafnir (Shotgun)Dragon's Breath: 40% Burn chance per shot, damage scales with half your Status bonus. Free Season Pass track. Fafnir guide
Iron Will (Chest)Resolved: body shots count as headshots, 2s PvE / 3s PvP cooldown, MMR/Rifle/Pistol only. Free Season Pass track. Iron Will guide
Loaded for Bear (Gloves)Afterburn: stacks up to 20 per target, reload converts them at 2% weapon damage each. Climax Mission exclusive. Loaded for Bear guide

Determined Rework

Determined now behaves like Perfect Determined: a converted headshot kill no longer chains into another guaranteed headshot. The old chaining playstyle moves to the Iron Will chest, which frees the weapon talent slot — the intended home is exotic MMRs and high-end MMRs with other talents. Marksman Rifle sets and brands (Hotshot, Aces & Eights, MMR damage bonuses) were buffed to compensate.

Brand & Gear Set Changes

Shock Resistance, Health, Incoming Repairs and Swap Speed leave brand bonuses; Protection from Elites joins. Each weapon type now has at least one brand offering its bonus at 1 piece and another at 2 pieces. Going forward there are no new brand sets — expect named pieces with perfect talents on existing brands instead. Red-core sets plus Ortiz: Exuro and True Patriot were rebalanced around risk versus reward.

Season Pass at a Glance

Free track carries both launch exotics plus caches, outfits and VFX. The $9.99 pass adds a second Fafnir copy, an exotic weapon skin, dyes, caches and premium credits. Full reward tables live on Ubisoft's announcement page linked above.

What This Means for Builds

Status-effect fire builds gain a real shotgun option, MMR builds rebuild around Iron Will instead of Determined, and glove slots stay open until the Climax Mission lands. Test combinations in the build planner and compare with tested builds.

FAQ

When did Red Horizon launch?
Year 8 Season 3 launched on August 27, 2026, bringing the Cleaners to Washington DC with a new global modifier.
What changed with Determined in Red Horizon?
Determined now behaves like Perfect Determined, so chained headshots no longer retrigger. The old chaining moved to the Iron Will exotic chest.
Which exotics are new in Red Horizon?
Fafnir shotgun and Iron Will chest on the free Season Pass track, plus Loaded for Bear gloves as the Climax Mission exclusive.
