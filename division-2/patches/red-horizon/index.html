The Division 2 / Y8S3 Update

Division 2 Red Horizon Update Guide

Year 8 Season 3 launched on August 27, 2026. The Cleaners push into Washington DC under a new global modifier, three exotics join the loot pool, and the Determined talent gets its biggest rework in years. Below is our plain-language summary — numbers are facts from the official notes, sentences are ours. Full texts: Ubisoft's Red Horizon patch notes.

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · Covers Y8S3 launch and the Y8S3.1 title update

Unofficial fan summary. Game content belongs to Ubisoft; full texts linked above.

Season Theme

Cleaners bring fire to DC. The seasonal modifier fills a Pressure Gauge through kills and unlocks active modifiers that no longer share cooldowns. A first-ever non-normalized Dark Zone variant (Blackout) keeps SHD Watch and Expertise power intact.

Dark Zone Overhaul

The Dark Zone changed more in Red Horizon than in any prior season. Variants rotate weekly across DZ West, East, and South with one PvE and two PvP zones live at any time. Toxic and Balanced stay in rotation every week, while Blackout and Classic alternate. Both new loot systems below pull from the same special Dark Zone pool: Exclusive and Raid gear sets, legacy DZ-exclusive weapons, Named items, and Exotics.

Toxic Dark Zone (PvE, permanent)Kept permanently after Into the Dark, with the seasonal Modifiers gone. Every enemy faction appears, including Black Tusk. Sample Canisters and Surge were removed, toxicity values were adjusted, and extracting your gear now clears your Toxicity level.
Toxic normalizationPvP stat normalization no longer applies, so talents, gear sets, skills and weapons use their PvE values against enemies. Prototype Gear stays normalized, matching Raids and Incursions.
Toxic rewardsLandmark chests have a 20% chance to drop a contaminated cache from the special Dark Zone pool.
Blackout Dark Zone (PvP, new)The first fully uncapped Dark Zone. Stat normalization is removed entirely, so SHD Watch and Expertise bonuses apply at full strength. The zone is permanently Invaded with Black Tusk as the main enemy, and Veteran, Elite and Named enemies hit harder. Global PvP Balance and Rogue Protocol stay active.
Blackout rewardsLoot drops at Heroic quality with a 20% chance of an additional contaminated item. Ortiz: Reficere, Caduceus and Nurse's Kneepads were added to the PvP special pool.
Prototype dropsContaminated Prototype gear and weapons drop from enemies: 0.2% mid rolls from normal enemies, 0.5% mid plus 0.2% high from elites, and 1% mid plus 2% high from bosses.
Global PvP BalanceAnother pass across Skills, gear set and brand set bonuses, and talents, applying to Conflict and every PvP Dark Zone variant. Red Horizon also added Ortiz: Exuro and True Patriot to the gear set rebalance.

Dark Zone farming details, current rotation rules and footage live on the Dark Zone guide.

Season Activities

95 Company (Elite Kill Squad)New season-limited Cleaner elite squad. It can invade other faction Retaliations in the same slot as past squads like BTSU Team 9 — a tougher named encounter rather than a guaranteed spawn.
Cleaner RetaliationsCleaners become the main Retaliation threat this season, taking Control Points and starting Retaliations in Named Zones. During other faction Retaliations they passively build Agitation instead of Black Tusk, which changes how fights escalate when more than one faction is pulled in.
Retaliation AnniversaryA dated Project Chain ran September 8-22 for the one-year mark, paying materials and caches per completed Project and ending in a choice outfit unlock. Progress bugs brought it back for one extra week from September 24 with hourly instead of daily unlocks.
The District Mall InfernoClassified Assignment, live August 27. Agent Felix McNally is attacked while recovering broad-spectrum antivirals, with the Cleaners closing in and an unknown assailant tied to an unaccounted SHD anomaly.
The Georgetown Dead ZoneSecond Classified Assignment, dated October 15. A JTF engineer team sent to install new tech in a stretch of Georgetown with no SHD network coverage has gone missing. Both unlock at Level 30 from the Assignments tab on the map.
CrossplayAnnounced at Gamescom for November 2026, following a partial rebuild of the online architecture. It will be on by default and can be disabled from the Character Selection screen. Exact date, supported platforms and progression model are not yet published.

Red Moon Rising (Y8S3.1)

The season's first title update landed on September 23, 2026 and added a separate PvE Dark Zone event running September 24 through November 3. It brought the Vertigo and Sarru exotics, the Rage Harvest return and further PvP tuning — see the Red Moon Rising guide.

New Exotics

Fafnir (Shotgun)Dragon's Breath: 40% Burn chance per shot, damage scales with half your Status bonus. Free Season Pass track. Fafnir guide
Iron Will (Chest)Resolved: body shots count as headshots, 2s PvE / 3s PvP cooldown, MMR/Rifle/Pistol only. Free Season Pass track. Iron Will guide
Loaded for Bear (Gloves)Afterburn: stacks up to 20 per target, reload converts them at 2% weapon damage each. Climax Mission exclusive. Loaded for Bear guide

Determined Rework

Determined now behaves like Perfect Determined: a converted headshot kill no longer chains into another guaranteed headshot. The old chaining playstyle moves to the Iron Will chest, which frees the weapon talent slot — the intended home is exotic MMRs and high-end MMRs with other talents. Marksman Rifle sets and brands (Hotshot, Aces & Eights, MMR damage bonuses) were buffed to compensate.

Brand & Gear Set Changes

Shock Resistance, Health, Incoming Repairs and Swap Speed leave brand bonuses; Protection from Elites joins. Each weapon type now has at least one brand offering its bonus at 1 piece and another at 2 pieces. Going forward there are no new brand sets — expect named pieces with perfect talents on existing brands instead. Red-core sets plus Ortiz: Exuro and True Patriot were rebalanced around risk versus reward.

Season Pass at a Glance

Free track carries both launch exotics plus caches, outfits and VFX. The $9.99 pass adds a second Fafnir copy, an exotic weapon skin, dyes, caches and premium credits. Full reward tables live on Ubisoft's announcement page linked above.

What This Means for Builds

Status-effect fire builds gain a real shotgun option, MMR builds rebuild around Iron Will instead of Determined, and glove slots stay open until the Climax Mission lands. Test combinations in the build planner and compare with tested builds.

FAQ

When did Red Horizon launch?
Year 8 Season 3 launched on August 27, 2026, bringing the Cleaners to Washington DC with a new global modifier.
What is the Blackout Dark Zone?
Blackout is the first fully uncapped Dark Zone. Stat normalization is removed entirely, so SHD Watch and Expertise bonuses apply at full strength. The zone is permanently Invaded by Black Tusk and drops Heroic loot with a 20% chance of an additional contaminated item.
Is the Toxic Dark Zone permanent now?
Yes. Toxic DZ became a permanent PvE variant in Red Horizon. Sample Canisters and Surge were removed, toxicity values were adjusted, extracting gear clears your Toxicity level, and PvP normalization no longer applies inside it.
What changed with Determined in Red Horizon?
Determined now behaves like Perfect Determined, so chained headshots no longer retrigger. The old chaining moved to the Iron Will exotic chest.
Which exotics are new in Red Horizon?
Fafnir shotgun and Iron Will chest on the free Season Pass track, plus Loaded for Bear gloves as the Climax Mission exclusive. Vertigo and Sarru arrived later, in the Y8S3.1 Red Moon Rising update.
When is crossplay coming?
Ubisoft announced crossplay for November 2026 at Gamescom, on by default and disableable from the Character Selection screen. The exact date, supported platforms and progression model are not yet published.
What are the Classified Assignments in Red Horizon?
The District Mall Inferno opened on August 27 and The Georgetown Dead Zone is dated October 15. Both unlock at Level 30 from the Assignments tab on the map.
