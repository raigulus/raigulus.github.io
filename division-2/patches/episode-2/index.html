Division 2 Episode 2 Guide

Pentagon: The Last Castle launched on October 15, 2019 as Title Update 6 — a race against the Black Tusk into America's most guarded building, wrapped in the biggest systems patch of Year 1. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

New Missions

Pentagon Breach opens the door, then The Pentagon and DARPA Research Labs chase a perfusion bioreactor through an underground lab the Black Tusk already infiltrated. Pass owners entered day one, everyone else a week later. See missions hub.

Technician & Loot

Technician (Specialization)Missile-launcher skill support with the Artificer Hive buffing and repairing your whole team's skills.
Sawyer's Kneepads (Exotic)Cover-to-cover protection for the always-moving agent.

Systems Overhaul

Recalibration grew up — cross-color stats, any-brand talent slots, new UI — beside crafting upgrades, bigger material caps, 300 stash slots, named-item chevrons, 12 loadouts, Kenly College mastery, DZ normalization and auto Shepherd endorsements. The second raid was deliberately held back to cook longer.

FAQ

When did Episode 2 launch?
Pentagon: The Last Castle launched on October 15, 2019 as Title Update 6, opening the Pentagon and DARPA missions.
What changed about recalibration in TU6?
Stats could move across colors, talents could slot onto any same-slot item regardless of brand, all inside a brand-new UI.
