The Division 2 / Title Update (TU4)

Division 2 TU4 Guide

Title Update 4 launched on June 18, 2019 — light on missions, heavy on identity: the game's first post-launch specialization finally gave tank players a home. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Gunner Specialization

A portable minigun that rewards never letting go of the trigger — kills without release feed ammo back to you and your squad. Bonus armor scales with carried spec ammo, but the gun stays out: no cover, vaulting or rolling while drawn. Banshee pulse confuses enemies out of cover, Riot Foam glues them down, and the P320 sidearm backs it up. Pass owners unlocked instantly; everyone else ran five Field Research stages. It set the template every later specialization copied.

New Loot

Dodge City Gunslinger (Exotic Holster)The first true exotic gear quest: buy the blueprint, run the chain, quickdraw for stacking damage.

Dark Zone & Clans

Going Rogue in the Occupied Zone got deadlier and fairer, generic-mod cheesing was closed, and small clans finally got reachable weekly projects with cheaper caches.

FAQ

When did Title Update 4 launch?
Title Update 4 launched on June 18, 2019, headlined by the Gunner specialization and the first true exotic gear quest.
How do you unlock the Gunner?
Year 1 Pass owners unlocked it instantly; everyone else completes a five-stage Special Field Research challenge chain.
