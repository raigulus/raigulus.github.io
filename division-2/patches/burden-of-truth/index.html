The Division 2 / Y6S3 Update

Division 2 Burden of Truth Update Guide

Year 6 Season 3 launched on February 25, 2025 with agents hunting the missing Alani Kelso through scout manhunts, plus kill-streak modifiers, a guaranteed exotic on the first Master Climax clear, and more stash space. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Kelso Manhunt

The season story follows Kelso deep into Keener's orbit. Weekly scout missions build toward a Climax Mission, and clearing it on Master for the first time guarantees an exotic drop — lower difficulties can still drop it at worse odds.

Streak Modifiers

A high-risk global modifier rewards fast chained kills with escalating buffs, while the hostile side grows meaner the further you push your seasonal journey. Active and passive modifiers unlock earlier than in past seasons, and open-world activities now feed seasonal XP directly, speeding up the pass.

QoL & Cosmetics

Stash space grew again, and March anniversary cosmetics celebrated the game's birthday. Active and passive modifier unlocks arrive through journey progression rather than late-season gates.

What This Means for Builds

Streak-friendly weapons and fast-clear setups win this season — test them in the build planner and run current content in today's targeted loot.
