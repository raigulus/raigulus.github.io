The Division 2 / Y7S2 Update

Division 2 The Pact Update Guide

Year 7 Season 2 launched on September 9, 2025 (patch 2.10) as True Sons, Outcasts and Hyenas forged an alliance — and answered with the permanent Retaliation activity plus exotic crafting. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Retaliation Activity

Retaliation triggers when a faction is sufficiently provoked: it marks a named zone and seizes every control point inside it. Retake them all before time runs out. Side Maneuver challenges buy extra time, Kill Squads raise the stakes, and an agitation meter per faction — pushed up by kills, missions and captures, or instantly by a daily project — decides who retaliates next. The mode stayed in the game permanently after the season.

Exotic Crafting

Retaliation materials (Hyena spice, Outcast canisters and equivalents) combine with Exotic Components to craft exotics, with better blueprint odds on higher difficulties. Full Retaliation clears can also yield raid and Dark Zone exotic blueprints.

Modifiers

The Divide and Conquer global modifier ties combat power to map control — bonuses shift with whichever faction holds each named zone — backed by faction-flavored active modifiers that level up through use.

FAQ

When did The Pact launch?
Year 7 Season 2 launched on September 9, 2025 with patch 2.10, adding the permanent Retaliation activity and exotic crafting.
What is Retaliation in The Division 2?
A permanent open-world activity where factions retaliate zone by zone. Retaking control points earns faction materials used to craft exotics.
Which exotics came with The Pact?
The Shroud marksman rifle and Overdogs gloves through crafting, plus the Tempest full-auto pistol from the Climax Mission.
