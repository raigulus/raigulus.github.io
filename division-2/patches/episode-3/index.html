Division 2 Episode 3 Guide

Coney Island: The Hunt launched on February 12, 2020 as Title Update 7 for pass holders, free for everyone on February 19 — a two-mission prologue that walks you straight into the expansion landing three weeks later. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Cure Hunt

Virologist Vitaly Tchernenko resurfaced in Black Tusk hands, and he may be the key to mass-producing antivirals. The trail runs through the Coney Island Ballpark and Amusement Park against returned Cleaners with new leadership — our channel has walked both. See missions hub. Pass holders also got two Classified Assignments; rat-catchers found a secret boss and a horror-cabinet Hunter mask.
