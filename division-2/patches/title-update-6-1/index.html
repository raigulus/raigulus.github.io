Division 2 TU6.1 Guide

Title Update 6.1 launched on December 10, 2019 — a holiday-sized patch hiding a permadeath bombshell. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Situation Snowball

Santa-hat hostiles roam the winter streets dropping the Sleigher Tommy Gun, wrapped in an apparel event with four outfits, emotes, skins and a mask. Thin but festive — exactly what a gloomy D.C. needed.

Hardcore Beta

One life, zero respawns: a fresh character in its own slot plays the entire game — matchmaking, Dark Zone (hardcore-only lobbies), even the raid — until death wipes it. Survivors flex the level-2 YOLO hat. Our channel's hardcore runs show how fast it ends. Beta on purpose: consoles tested alongside PC instead of waiting for a PTS cycle.

Guaranteed StockTwo rotating named items plus a permanent Shield Splinterer for Ivory Key holders.
