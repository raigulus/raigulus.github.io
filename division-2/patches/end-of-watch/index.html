The Division 2 / Y1S4 Update

Division 2 End of Watch Update Guide

Year 1 Season 4 launched on December 8, 2020 to end the first seasonal year with a gut punch: your own former commander, now rogue, leading a Black Tusk-recruited cell. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Lau Manhunt

Prime target Faye Lau brings four rogue lieutenants — Viper, Kestrel, Magnus and Circe — and the trail runs from D.C. to Camp White Oak, where the confrontation plants the seeds for everything Year 4 later reveals about her. Target order on our manhunts hub.

New Loot

Capacitor (Exotic AR)Skill battery: stacks raise skill damage while each skill tier feeds damage back into the rifle.
Scorpio (Exotic Shotgun)Fast-firing status stacker that opens with poison and ends with raw damage — a close-range legend to this day.
Waveform (Exotic Holster)Capacitor's bigger sibling: banks even more skill damage per stack.
Rigger (Set)The skill set: quicker deployment, deadlier skills.
Empress International (Brand)Skill brand debuting alongside, with backpack and chest named pieces.

Summit & Skills

The Summit skyscraper got targeted-loot checkpoints and a fresh objective category, the Achilles Pulse learned to paint weak points, and next-gen consoles got their frame-rate lift in the season's second half.
