Division 2 Warlords of New York Guide

The expansion launched on March 3, 2020 as Title Update 8 — back to where it all began, hunting the First Rogue through a hurricane-hit Manhattan. It also rebooted the entire endgame underneath the campaign. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Keener Campaign

A bio-attack levels City Hall and the trail leads to Lower Manhattan: four rogue warlords — Theo Parnell, James Dragov, Javier Kajika and Vivian Conley — each holding a zone, with Keener waiting on Liberty Island. New Haven base, no district level gates, and yes, a puppy. The seasonal manhunts that followed all build on this hunt — see manhunts hub.

New Endgame

Level 40 & SHD LevelsCap rises 30 to 40 with a free boost, then infinite SHD points across Offense, Defense, Utility, Misc and endless Scavenging — your watch face shows it off.
SeasonsThree-month arcs with manhunts, leagues, events and a reward track. Season 1 Shadow Tide started one week later — see our Shadow Tide guide.
Legendary DifficultyStrongholds at Roosevelt, Capitol and DUA guarded by an elite Black Tusk sub-faction — the hardest PvE outside raids.
Gear 2.0Every brand, set and exotic reworked; Skill Power removed; clearer stats; crafting bench scales to your level.

FAQ

When did Warlords of New York launch?
The expansion launched on March 3, 2020 as Title Update 8, taking agents to Lower Manhattan to hunt Aaron Keener.
What are SHD levels?
The infinite progression system past level 40: points go into Offense, Defense, Utility and Misc stats, then into endless Scavenging for materials.
