FAQ

What guides are on this page?
This hub covers Division 2 patch notes summaries, no commentary mission walkthroughs (invaded, heroic, legendary), and boss encounter guides including secret boss triggers.
Are the mission walkthroughs no commentary?
Yes. All mission walkthrough pages on this hub are no commentary unless labeled otherwise. Commentary, speedrun, or archive clips are labeled separately.
Are these the official patch notes?
No. These are unofficial fan-written summaries. Numbers are facts from the official notes; sentences are our own. Always check Ubisoft's pages for the full text.
Can I follow boss guides solo?
Some boss routes can be followed solo, but your difficulty, build, and encounter knowledge still matter.
Do these pages claim all loot or collectibles?
No. The pages avoid all loot, all SHD caches, and guaranteed drop claims unless a video explicitly proves them.
