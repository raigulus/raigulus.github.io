The Division 2 / Y6S1 Update

Division 2 First Rogue Update Guide

Year 6 Season 1 launched on June 11, 2024 with Aaron Keener alive in Washington DC, demanding four trials before any parley — plus the One Endgame unification that opened level 40 to every owner. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

One Endgame

Every owner now levels to 40 without the expansion: seasons, Summit, Countdown, Paradise Lost and all level-40 loot opened up. World Tiers were removed, gear score went away, specializations unlock at 30, and item power runs one clean scale from 1 to 40.

Keener's Trials

The manhunt runs through four trials — including the Hunter-leak revelations around Camp White Oak and a crashed parley at the Federal Emergency Bunker — with Kelso's defection to Keener hanging over everything.
