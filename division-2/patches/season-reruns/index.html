The Division 2 / Seasons 5-8

Division 2 Season Reruns Guide

Through 2021 into early 2022 the game replayed its first four seasons in order instead of writing new ones, no new prime targets, no new loot, just second chances. One page covers all four, because four thin pages would serve nobody. Details on each original run live in their own guides, linked below.

Why It Matters

Missed the EMP Sticky Bomb, the Shrapnel Trap or the Healing Trap the first time? The reruns were the catch-up window, and the quiet before Season 9's Hidden Alliance rebooted everything with Countdown and Expertise.

FAQ

Why did The Division 2 re-run old seasons?
The team paused original seasons through 2021 into early 2022 while building the next era, replaying Seasons 1-4 as Seasons 5-8.
Do rerun seasons have new rewards?
No. Reruns replay the original manhunts, leagues and events, so veterans chase anything they missed and newcomers meet old prime targets.
