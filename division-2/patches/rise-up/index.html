The Division 2 / Y8S1 Update

Division 2 Rise Up Update Guide

Year 8 Season 1 launched in early April 2026 (patch 2.24) and raised the endgame ceiling: a new Escalation difficulty, a Prototype gear rarity, Black Tusk Retaliation events and the return of Classified Assignments. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Covers launch + Y8S1.2

Escalation Difficulty

Escalation is a permanent endgame replay mode with 10 tiers, token-based entry and no checkpoints — fail the run and the wagered tokens are gone. Five missions and strongholds rotate every Tuesday, with seasonal mutators keeping the rotation fresh. Solo, group and matchmaking are all supported from level 40.

Prototype Rarity

Prototype sits above existing tiers with stat values up to roughly 1.5 times previous limits, plus an Augment system (over 30 passive modifiers) fed by Prototype Cores. Pieces drop in Escalation or upgrade from high-end, gear-set and named items — but Prototype power is limited in PvP, raids and incursions by design.

Retaliation & Modifiers

Black Tusk Retaliation events fill an agitation meter through kills and activities, with Maneuver bonus objectives and rare Kill Squads — including the elite BTSU Team 9 — dropping blueprints for Climax, raid and Dark Zone exotics. The Recombinant global modifier plus the Back and Forth talent family reward swapping weapons mid-fight.

Y8S1.2 Mid-Season Patch (May 12)

The mid-season update added the Escalation Requisition Vendor (three Prototype caches for tokens), the Capitol Globe Anomaly classified assignment, Assault and Ambush global events, a Vanguard Overdrive multiplier week, and a long bug-fix list including Prototype sharing and Danny Weaver cache purchases.

What This Means for Builds

Weapon-swap talents made aggressive play pay — test Agitator pairings in the build planner and run the weekly set in Escalation Tier 10.
