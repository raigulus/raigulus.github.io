The Division 2 / Y1S2 Update

Division 2 Keener's Legacy Update Guide

Year 1 Season 2 launched on June 23, 2020 with Title Update 10 — the season the original villain stepped out of the shadows himself, and a True Sons foundry became the game's second raid. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Keener Manhunt

Prime target Aaron Keener splintered the story across five lieutenants — Hornet, Termite, Luna, Huntsman and Titan — each lending their name to a league. Clearing all five unlocks the Healing Trap skill variant. Target order on our manhunts hub.

Operation Iron Horse

The True Sons seized a foundry to build weapons of mass disruption, and eight agents answer. Two raid-exclusive exotics wait inside: the double-barrel Ravenous that swaps primers per shoulder, and the Regulus magnum turning headshot kills into five-meter explosions. Full raid notes on our Incursions & Raids hub.

Rebalance Pass

The season's quiet giant: nearly every weapon, most gear, NPCs and crowd-control got retuned for game health, loot generosity and player power — the first of the great rebalance eras.
