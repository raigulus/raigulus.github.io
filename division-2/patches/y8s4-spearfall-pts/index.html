Division 2 Y8S4 Spearfall PTS Patch Notes

Ubisoft's Spearfall Public Test Server notes landed on November 12, 2026. The PC test ran November 13-16, 2026, covering Season Modifiers, a wider Legendary difficulty rollout, Escalation reworks, Prototype Gear changes, and new loot. This is our plain-language summary; the full text is on Ubisoft's official patch notes.

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Covers Y8S4 PTS

Unofficial fan summary. Game content belongs to Ubisoft; full notes linked above.

Test Window

PTS openedFriday, November 13, 2026 at 2 PM CET / 8 AM ET / 5 AM PT
PTS closedMonday, November 16, 2026 at 2 PM CET / 8 AM ET / 5 AM PT
PlatformPC only, using a separate test client and save template

Season Modifiers

Enemy factions carry counterfeit Prototype Gear Augments. Some enemies gain a Hostile Modifier and a charging Contra-Augment. Deal enough damage to the highlighted weak point to cancel the charge; hitting elsewhere makes it fill faster. Cancels build seasonal progress and feed your own Active Modifier.

Unknown Technologies

The global Enhancement Track has five segments. Each starts at its lowest values and grows as you earn points from Hostile Modifier kills and Contra-Augment cancels. Segment V adds Augment Efficiency, which also strengthens Prototype Augment effects that scale with weapon level.

Active and Passive Modifiers

You can equip one of three Active Modifiers once all five Frequency Pattern slots are full and you have personally cancelled a Contra-Augment:

Advanced LethalityChance on damage to grant a temporary Damage to Armor bonus.
Mirrored SabotageUses the last Contra-Augment you cancelled to weaken or disrupt enemies.
Mirrored BoostGrants a personal buff based on the last Contra-Augment you cancelled.

Eleven Passive Modifiers split into five Augmented Track boosts and six Tweaks that change Pattern gains, boot speed, duration, and jammer behaviour. Hostile Countermeasures for Quantum, Echo, Amalgam and Anomaly activate if an enemy finishes charging.

Legendary Difficulty

Four Main Missions gain a Legendary option with reworked White Tusk encounters, and Legendary becomes a Global Difficulty in Washington, D.C. only:

New Legendary missionsAir & Space Museum, Space Administration HQ, Grand Washington Hotel, ViewPoint Museum
Open worldWhite Tusk replaces Black Tusk in D.C. activities; other factions get increased health and damage. No bounty drops in Legendary Open World.
ProjectsThe daily Legendary Mission Project refreshes daily; three daily projects in a week award a larger loot package.
RewardsPrototype Gear drops from the new Legendary missions during the test, with temporary drop rates.

Legendary applies only to the Main Mission version of those four missions. Heroic remains the highest difficulty for Retaliation.

Escalation Updates

You now choose a Directive and a Tier separately before a run. The run timer is removed across all Directives and Tiers, and a personal Player Rating grades performance and improves your Prototype Gear chances. Tier unlocks require finishing a run at that Tier's maximum Rating. Matchmaking considers both your Directive and Tier.

MarkedMark a target for bonus damage; Hunter stacks build from kills and sidearm shots consume stacks to Ensnare.
Core BuildRed, Blue or Yellow stacks based on your dominant Core colour, each with a group ability at full stacks.
CollaborationRole-focused play with Resolve and Wounded, plus the Active Mesh enemy mutator.

General Rating sources include fast consecutive kills, headshot or grenade kills, kills from cover, revives, healing, Smart Cover buffs and weak-point destroys. Getting downed lowers your Rating.

Prototype Gear

Augment rerolls are replaced by direct selection in the ProtoLab, with a fixed Prototype Core cost. Augment level now belongs to the item, so switching Augments keeps your invested progress. Upgrading an item to Prototype quality requires Expertise Level 30.

Prototype Schematics replace standard Recalibration and Optimization for Prototype items. A Schematic can add a bonus attribute, recalibrate a slot, stabilize an attribute value, or recalibrate a Talent. Named attributes and Talents are protected, Core Attributes including Skill Tier are ineligible in Y8S4, and each item can hold one Schematic at a time. Most Schematics come from the Prototype Requisition Vendor; others unlock through missions or Legendary bosses. Prototype Cores can also be donated in the Expertise menu for Proficiency.

New Gear and Weapons

August (Exotic AR)Builds Convergent stacks on body hits; reloading at full stacks grants Accuracy and Headshot Damage for a short window.
Forward Observer (Exotic Mask)Aiming at an enemy pulses and marks them, adding amplified damage and repairs on kill.
Field General (Gear Set)Weapon Handling and Armor bonuses, plus Helping Hand marks on enemies damaged by allies.
Named itemsMagnet, Siphon, Thrive, Hearty, Blackout X-45, Power Spike 1886, and the Technophobia Talent.

For exact values, see the official PTS notes and Ubisoft's linked Y8S4 balance articles.

PTS Test Only and Known Issues

The test client includes a save template with maxed Prototype Gear and Named Items, temporary Legendary drop rates, and all modifiers unlocked from the start. Ubisoft asked for feedback on Legendary enemy tuning and noted that browser issues affect Marked and Collaboration Escalation Directives, HUD Rating displays, enemy scaling, and some loot events in Legendary Open World. Check the official notes for the full Known Issues list.
