Division 2 Shadow Tide Update Guide

Year 1 Season 1 ran from March 10 to June 15, 2020 — the experiment that invented the seasonal model: a 12-week track, leagues, global events, apparel event and one rogue cell to dismantle piece by piece. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Jupiter Manhunt

With Keener down and the Rogue Network lit, Molly "Jupiter" Henderson's cell plots to EMP the Division into the dark. Four lieutenants — Neptune, Venus, Mercury, plus Saturn hiding the New York safehouse — lead to a retaken Roosevelt Island held by boat-landed Cleaners. The finale hands you the EMP Sticky Bomb variant. Target order on our manhunts hub — and our channel holds a solo flawless of that very island.

Seasonal Model

Four leagues, three global events, the Urban Jungle apparel event and a season-level reward track: every structure later seasons reused was prototyped here.

FAQ

When did Shadow Tide launch?
Year 1 Season 1 ran from March 10 to June 15, 2020, introducing the seasonal model with the Jupiter manhunt.
What do you unlock for finishing the Jupiter manhunt?
Clearing all five rogue agents unlocks the EMP Sticky Bomb variant, recovered from Jupiter's jammer plot on Roosevelt Island.
