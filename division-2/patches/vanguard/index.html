The Division 2 / Y5S3 Update

Division 2 Vanguard Update Guide

Year 5 Season 3 launched on February 6, 2024 (patch 1.64) with agents tracking the missing Alani Kelso across New York, alongside Project Resolve, the biggest stability overhaul in years. Our plain-language summary below: full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Unofficial fan summary. Game content belongs to Ubisoft; full texts linked above.

Kelso Manhunt

Unlike prior manhunts chasing a roster of targets, this one follows a single thread: find Kelso through Places of Interest across New York, with the trail pointing toward Keener's orbit and setting up Year 6.

Project Resolve

Thousands of bug fixes plus lower latency, fewer crashes and disconnects, PvP rebalancing, the Tinkering station merging recalibration and optimization, and a full weapon rebalance pass.
