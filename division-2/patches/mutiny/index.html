The Division 2 / Y7S3 Update

Division 2 Mutiny Update Guide

Year 7 Season 3 launched on December 2, 2025 (patch 2.15) as True Sons defectors broke ranks against General Anderson — and for the first time you can recruit them as NPC Companions fighting beside you. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Covers launch patch

NPC Companions & Command Link

Defectors met in open-world encounters and Mutiny events can be recruited if they survive the fight. As squad leader you order them to reposition, focus targets, support the team or trigger abilities through the Command Link global talent, which pays out stacking buffs — crit damage, reload speed, weapon damage and bonus armor — plus link points per order type. New Commander, Jammer and Fireteam enemy behaviors exist specifically to break up that coordination.

What This Means for Builds

Everything this season scales with allies — group members, NPCs, skills and your Companion all count. Test aura-stacking setups in the build planner and run current content in today's targeted loot.

FAQ

When did Mutiny launch?
Year 7 Season 3 launched on December 2, 2025 with patch 2.15, adding recruitable NPC Companions.
How do Companions work in Mutiny?
Defected True Sons soldiers met in open-world encounters can be recruited, then ordered to move, focus targets, support or use abilities through Command Link.
Which exotics came with Mutiny?
The Bittersweet marksman rifle and Beacon chest through seasonal play, plus the Sheriff shotgun from the Master Climax Mission.
