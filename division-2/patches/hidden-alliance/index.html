The Division 2 / Y4S1 Update

Division 2 Hidden Alliance Update Guide

Year 4 Season 1 launched on May 12, 2022 in the shadow of Camp White Oak — with Faye Lau gone, her encrypted comms hinting she was undercover all along, and the True Sons mutating under new leadership. This season also rewrote how manhunts tell stories. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Lewis Manhunt

Prime target Captain Lewis marches on D.C. with his own agenda while Ridgeway's death fractures the True Sons into zealots and opportunists. Four officers — Castilla, Chang, Daniels and Xander — each seize a campaign mission as their turf. Kelso now debriefs you in person at the White House so no story beat gets lost. Target order on our manhunts hub.

Countdown & Expertise

The season's quiet giants launched here, not next season: the 8-player Countdown power-plant run, and the Expertise system that lets you permanently upgrade favorite weapons and gear by using them. Both outlived every season around them.

New Loot

Dread Edict (Exotic MMR)Full Stop: body shots stack damage up to twenty, headshots double-stack, and reloading converts the pile into temporary armor — headshot kills skip the reload entirely.
Catharsis (Exotic Mask)Vicious Cycle: eating damage charges you up, and maxing out purges everything into a healing cloud for the squad.
Bluescreen (Exotic LMG)Disruptor Rounds: paint enemies to bank stacks up to fifty. Blueprint from the Lewis manhunt, refills from Countdown LMG loot — our channel build shows the playstyle.
Heartbreaker (Set)Headshots pulse victims while follow-up hits stack bonus armor and damage up to fifty — double with chest and backpack.
Uzina Getica (Brand)Tanky brand with flat armor, armor-on-kill and hazard protection.

Storytelling Shift

Cinematics, Base of Operations debriefs and mission-takeover lieutenants made this the first season that plays like episodes — the template every later season copied.

FAQ

When did Hidden Alliance launch?
Year 4 Season 1 launched on May 12, 2022, picking up right after the Camp White Oak showdown with Faye Lau.
How do you get the Bluescreen exotic LMG?
Finish the Captain Lewis manhunt for the blueprint, then farm Countdown with LMG targeted loot for more rolls.
