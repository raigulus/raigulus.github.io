Division 2 Dark Hours Guide

Operation Dark Hours launched on May 16, 2019 as part of Title Update 3 — delayed three weeks past April so balance patches could land first. Eight agents, one hijacked airport, zero matchmaking: the raid era began here. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Airport Assault

The Black Tusk turned Washington National Airport into their supply pipeline, and only coordinated teams at World Tier 5 with high gear scores need apply. Bosses backed by the faction's best tech gate every hangar. Tactics live on our Incursions & Raids hub.

Race to World First

Launch morning opened with a broadcast race: the first clear team earned their photo and names on the White House wall, every finisher took a clan banner and trophy, and week-one clears added a commemorative patch.

FAQ

When did Operation Dark Hours launch?
The first 8-player raid launched on May 16, 2019 as part of Title Update 3, after a short delay for balance tuning.
How do you get the Eagle Bearer?
Eagle Bearer drops exclusively from Operation Dark Hours, rewarding sustained accurate fire with its Tenacity talent.
