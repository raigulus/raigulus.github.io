Division 2 Episode 1 Guide

D.C. Outskirts: Expeditions launched on July 23, 2019 as Title Update 5, the first paid-pass episode, pushing the map past the city limits in two directions. Our plain-language summary below: full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Unofficial fan summary. Game content belongs to Ubisoft; full texts linked above.

New Missions

Manning National Zoo sends you after Emeline Shaw and her Outcasts through animal enclosures turned warzone, while Camp White Oak digs into Black Tusk leadership in the woods. Both later became invaded staples and Legendary strongholds, our channel has a full Zoo walkthrough. See missions hub.

New Loot

Diamondback (Exotic Rifle)Lever-action mark-and-execute rifle with snake-venom stacking damage.
BTSU Datagloves (Exotic Gloves)Skill-haste gloves feeding grenade and hive uptime.
Carbine 7 / Stoner LMGNew assault rifle and light machine gun for the base arsenal.

Expeditions & Extras

Free-roam Expeditions wrapped the new zones in narrative collectibles and boss mechanics, Dark Hours gained a Story difficulty, Heroic bosses started dropping exotics, and pistols finally got flashlights.

FAQ

When did Episode 1 launch?
D.C. Outskirts: Expeditions launched on July 23, 2019 as Title Update 5, adding the Zoo and Camp White Oak missions.
Was the Gunner part of Episode 1?
No. The Gunner specialization and Dodge City Gunslinger arrived a month earlier in Title Update 4 (June 18, 2019).
