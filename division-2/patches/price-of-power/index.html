Division 2 Price of Power Update Guide

Year 4 Season 2 launched on September 13, 2022 with a power-hungry general stitching the True Sons and Black Tusk back together — while last season's Countdown mode matured into the game's best exotic farm. Our plain-language summary below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Covers launch patch

Anderson Manhunt

Prime target General Peter Anderson wants Ridgeway's chair and a reunited True Sons–Black Tusk alliance strong enough to bury the Division. Stopping him means cutting through his officers first. Target order on our manhunts hub.

Countdown Comes of Age

Born last season in Hidden Alliance, the Pentco power-plant run found its identity here: two fireteams of four against the clock, difficulties for every level, its own requisition vendor — and targeted loot that turned it into the exotic farm. See what's hot on our loot hub.

New Loot

Doctor Home (Exotic Rifle)Marks a target per shot; the kill drops an armor kit that heals the whole squad on pickup. Blueprint from the Anderson manhunt, refills from Countdown rifle loot.
Busy Little Bee (Exotic Pistol)Tag a different enemy per shot to stack damage, then swap weapons to cash the stacks in for ten seconds of pain.
Umbra Initiative (Set)Cover-dance set: bank stacks while tucked in, spend them pushing forward — crit, reload speed and armor regen included.
Brazos de Arcabuz (Brand)Skill builder's brand with haste, a free skill tier and bigger magazines.
Melee Gloves (Exotic)Grenade or melee openers trigger a twenty-second damage surge on a minute cooldown.

Rebalances

Ongoing Directive and Striker's Battlegear both got tuning passes in the same patch — the start of the long road to the modern DPS meta.

FAQ

When did Price of Power launch?
Year 4 Season 2 launched on September 13, 2022, bringing new loot, rebalances and the hunt for General Peter Anderson.
How does Countdown work?
Two teams of four enter a power plant against the clock, clear two of three objectives to unlock the main goal, then survive a hunter-grade ambush on the way out.
