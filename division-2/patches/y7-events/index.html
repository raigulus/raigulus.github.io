Division 2 Year 7 Seasonal Events

Between the big Year 7 seasons, smaller event patches kept the calendar full: Halloween, a Rank Up extension, a Krampus Winter event, Bloody Valentine and the Vanguard Overdrive multiplier. Our plain-language roundup below — full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Why These Matter Now

Ubisoft recycles event formats — Reanimated, global multipliers and holiday hunts return in new skins. Knowing how Rank Up stretch goals or a Houndsman hunt worked the first time is a head start next time. For current rotations see today's targeted loot.

FAQ

What events ran during Division 2 Year 7?
Halloween in autumn, a Rank Up stretch-goals extension in November, a Krampus Winter event across New Year, and a Bloody Valentine event in February.
What was Rank Up in Division 2?
A season-pass extension event running November 18 to December 2 with stretch goals beyond the normal track.
