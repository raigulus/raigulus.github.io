Division 2 Echoes of Central Park DLC Guide

Echoes of Central Park arrives November 3 as the darkest Division 2 DLC yet: a flashback story through Central Park and the Upper West Side, a new virus variant, an apex threat, companions, and new skills and gear. Our plain-language overview below: full texts live on Ubisoft's news hub.

Release Date

Echoes of Central Park releases on November 3. Agents below level 40 get a boost so they can start the DLC immediately, fully equipped for the challenge.

Flashback Story

A first for The Division 2: the story plays as a flashback, with a narrator guiding you through her version of events. Paranoia grips a society facing an unknown sinister threat, and fear feeds on itself until rumors become truth. Set in the summer after Warlords of New York, the hunt follows a distress signal from deep inside the park.

Map: Upper West Side & Central Park

The map covers Central Park and the Upper West Side from Lincoln Square to the Dakota District — larger than Battle for Brooklyn. The park has been reclaimed by nature after serving as a mass burial ground during the outbreak, and a new apex threat has taken residence in the shadows alongside familiar foes.

You can play Echoes of Central Park solo or in co-op. Below-level-40 agents receive a boost to jump straight in. Mission walkthroughs will land in the mission archive after release.

Finish the Red Moon Rising reward pools before they rotate, lock in a tested build from the build guides or the build planner, and stock up through today's targeted loot. Walkthroughs for the new missions land in the mission archive after release.
