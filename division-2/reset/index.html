FAQ

When does The Division 2 daily reset happen?
The daily reset happens every day at 08:00 UTC (11:00 TRT, 04:00 EDT in summer). Targeted loot, daily projects and related rotations refresh at that time.
When is The Division 2 weekly reset?
The weekly reset happens on Tuesdays around 08:00 UTC. Vendor stock, weekly projects, invasions and legendary rotations refresh together. Tuesday maintenance can shift the exact minute, so check the server status tracker on reset day.
When does the Division 2 incursion key chest reset?
The incursion key chest resets every Monday at 08:00 UTC, at the daily reset time.
When does Cassie Mendoza open?
Cassie Mendoza opens on Wednesdays once her location becomes available via the Snitch, one day after the Tuesday weekly vendor reset.
What time is The Division 2 reset in Turkey?
Turkey (TRT) stays fixed at UTC+3 all year, so the 08:00 UTC reset is always 11:00 in Turkey - no summer/winter shift.
