    About the Data

    Item data comes from the community-maintained Division 2 Community Data Hub and is refreshed after each game patch. Brand set bonuses, gear set bonuses, talents, and base weapon stats are included. Attribute rolls are shown as ranges; exact values depend on your in-game rolls.

    FAQ

    Can I share my build? Yes — use "Copy link" to share the exact build, or "Copy as text" for a Discord-friendly summary.

    Does the tool check exotic limits? Yes — it warns when more than one exotic weapon or one exotic gear piece is equipped.

    How fresh is the item data? The data file shows its generation date at the top of the page and is updated after patches.
