Red Horizon Dark Zone Changes

Toxic Dark Zone arrived in Into the Dark and became a permanent PvE fixture in Red Horizon. Variants now rotate weekly across DZ West, East, and South, with one PvE and two PvP Dark Zones active at any time. Toxic and Balanced stay in rotation every week, while Blackout and Classic alternate.

Toxic Dark Zone (PvE)Permanent since Red Horizon. Every enemy faction can appear, including Black Tusk. Sample Canisters and Surge were removed. Toxicity values were adjusted, and extracting your gear now clears your Toxicity level.
Toxic normalizationPvP stat normalization is no longer applied here, so talents, gear sets, skills, and weapons use their PvE values against enemies. Prototype Gear stays normalized, matching Raids and Incursions.
Toxic rewardsLandmark chests have a 20% chance to drop a contaminated cache from the special Dark Zone pool: Exclusive and Raid gear sets, legacy DZ-exclusive weapons, Named items, and Exotics.
Blackout Dark Zone (PvP)New uncapped PvP variant. Stat normalization is fully removed, so SHD Watch and Expertise bonuses apply at full strength. The zone is permanently Invaded with Black Tusk, and Veteran, Elite, and Named enemies are tougher.
Blackout rewardsLoot drops at Heroic quality, with a 20% chance of an additional contaminated item from the same special pool. Ortiz: Reficere, Caduceus, and Nurse's Kneepads were added to the PvP pool.
Prototype dropsContaminated Prototype gear and weapons drop from enemies: 0.2% mid rolls from normals, 0.5% mid plus 0.2% high from elites, and 1% mid plus 2% high from bosses.
Global PvP BalanceRed Horizon added another pass across Skills, gear set and brand set bonuses, and talents. These apply to Conflict and every PvP Dark Zone variant.
SourceRed Horizon (Y8S3) update guide · Ubisoft Red Horizon patch notes

Red Moon Rising: Vertigo and Sarru in the PvE Dark Zone

Red Moon Rising runs in the PvE Dark Zone from September 24 to November 3, 2026. Black Tusk takes over the zone, Red Moon Stacks change with every fight, and a new world boss rotates through it.

Vertigo and Sarru sourceThe XB-35 "Phoebe" Marauder world boss is the only source of both exotics during the event. Neither drop is guaranteed.
Event windowSeptember 24 - November 3, 2026.
Active zoneThe PvE Dark Zone rotates weekly across DZ East, South, and West. DZ South hosted week one.
Red Moon StacksStacks build through combat and raise rewards and risk together. Dying, leaving the zone, or extracting contaminated loot resets them. Safehouses pause the buildup.
Event exotic poolVertigo, Sarru, Steel & Sons, Fafnir, Iron Will, Bighorn, Eagle Bearer, and Ravenous.
Drop rateUbisoft has not published a drop percentage for either exotic, so no clear count is guaranteed.
After the eventBoth Vertigo and Sarru leave the event-specific pool once Red Moon Rising ends and return to the general exotic pool.
Also in the zoneThe returning Recruiter drops the Recruiter Mask cosmetic.
SourceY8S3.1 Red Moon Rising patch notes

What is Dark Zone Surge in The Division 2?

Dark Zone Surge is a limited event window in the Year 8 Season 2 schedule. For July 7-14, 2026, Ubisoft lists 3x Dark Zone XP and double DZ currency.

Is Toxic DZ good for farming?

Toxic DZ can be useful when you want PvE Dark Zone farming, personal loot visibility, and event checks, but build comfort and Hazard Protection still matter.

What should I watch before farming Dark Zone Surge?

Start with Toxic DZ first-look testing, the TUY8S2.1 Toxic DZ update short, and longer PvP or DZ archive clips to understand checkpoint pressure and combat pacing.

Where do Vertigo and Sarru drop in The Division 2?

Both drop from the XB-35 Phoebe Marauder world boss during the Red Moon Rising event in the PvE Dark Zone, between September 24 and November 3, 2026. Neither drop is guaranteed and Ubisoft has not published a drop rate.

What changed in the Dark Zone with Red Horizon?

Toxic Dark Zone became a permanent PvE variant with Sample Canisters and Surge removed, toxicity adjusted, and extraction clearing your Toxicity level. A new uncapped Blackout PvP Dark Zone joined the rotation. Variants rotate weekly with one PvE and two PvP zones active at a time.

Does this page replace official patch notes?

No. This page groups Raigulus Dark Zone videos and search context. Use official Ubisoft news for exact event dates and patch details.
