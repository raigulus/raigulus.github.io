Where Is Cassie Mendoza?

Unofficial fan guide. Cassie Mendoza is the secret weekly vendor: her location rotates every week and is revealed through the Snitch each Wednesday, one day after the Tuesday reset. For live countdowns, check the Division 2 reset times.

How to Find Her

After the Wednesday reset, find the Snitch in the open world. Talking to him marks Cassie Mendoza's shop on your map for the week. Her location changes with every rotation, so last week's spot will not work. Plan the trip together with today's targeted loot farm.

What She Sells

Cassie stocks rotating named items and caches that change with each weekly location. Check her full inventory every Wednesday before the next rotation, since sought-after rolls only stay for one week.
