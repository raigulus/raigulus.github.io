FAQ

What can I request from Raigulus?
Video requests work best with specifics: mission or boss name, difficulty, and build if you have one in mind. Error reports should include the page URL. Build ideas need gear, talents, and what the build is for.
How fast do you respond to feedback?
Discord is fastest for back-and-forth discussion. Form messages are read within a few days and popular requests become videos.
Can I report a wrong talent or stat on a guide?
Yes, those reports are the most valuable. Include the page URL and, if possible, a screenshot or video timestamp showing the correct value in game.
