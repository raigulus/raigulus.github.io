Tips for Lost Sectors

Tip 1

Check Champion Mods

Look at the modifiers before entering. Equip the right Anti-Champion mods for Barrier, Unstoppable, or Overload champions.

Tip 2

Solo for Exotics

You must complete the Lost Sector alone to get exotic engrams. Fireteam matchmaking will disable exotic drops.

Tip 3

Master for Better Odds

Master Lost Sectors have higher exotic drop rates but are significantly harder. Only attempt if your loadout is ready.

FAQ

What time is the Destiny 2 daily reset?
The Destiny 2 daily reset happens every day at 10:00 AM Pacific Time (PT), which is 17:00 UTC. Lost Sector rotations change at this time.
How often do Lost Sectors rotate in Destiny 2?
Lost Sectors rotate daily at the daily reset. Each destination (EDZ, Cosmodrome, Moon, Europa, Throne World) has its own rotation cycle.
What is the difference between Legend and Master Lost Sectors?
Legend Lost Sectors are easier with fewer champions and shields. Master Lost Sectors are harder with more champions and shields but offer better exotic drop rates. Both drop exotic engrams when completed solo.
Do I need to unlock Lost Sectors first?
Yes, you must first discover and complete the Lost Sector on any character before it can appear in the daily Legend/Master rotation.
