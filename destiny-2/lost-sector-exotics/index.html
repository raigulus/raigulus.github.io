Destiny 2 / Lost Sector Exotics

Destiny 2 Lost Sector Exotic Drops

Unofficial fan guide. Legend and Master Lost Sectors are the solo path to exotic armor: finish alone and an exotic engram can drop, with Master roughly doubling the odds. For today's rotation, check the Destiny 2 Lost Sector tracker.

Legend vs Master Drop Rates

Legend CompletionExotic Engram (solo only), Enhancement Cores, Glimmer
Master CompletionExotic Engram (higher drop rate), Enhancement Cores, Glimmer
Exotic Drop Rate~25% Legend, ~50% Master (approximate)
Solo RequiredYes - must complete alone for exotic drops

Which Difficulty Should You Farm?

Master has better exotic drop rates but is significantly harder, with more champions and shields. Farm Legend if your clears are twice as fast: more completions per hour beats a higher per-run rate. Only step up to Master when your loadout handles the champion density comfortably.

Champion Modifiers

Champion TypesVary by Lost Sector (Barrier, Unstoppable, Overload)
Anti-Champion ModsEquip the right mods for the champions in rotation
ShieldsMaster adds more shielded enemies - match elements

Rotation and Unlock Rules

Lost Sectors rotate daily at 10:00 AM Pacific Time (17:00 UTC), and each destination - EDZ, Cosmodrome, Moon, Europa, Throne World - runs its own rotation cycle. You must first discover and complete a Lost Sector on any character before it can appear in the daily Legend and Master rotation.

Tips for Exotic Farming

Tip 1

Check Champion Mods

Look at the modifiers before entering. Equip the right Anti-Champion mods for Barrier, Unstoppable, or Overload champions.

Tip 2

Solo for Exotics

You must complete the Lost Sector alone to get exotic engrams. Fireteam matchmaking will disable exotic drops.

Tip 3

Speed Beats Difficulty

A fast Legend clear loop earns more exotics per hour than slow Master attempts. Time your runs and pick the difficulty with the best clears-per-hour rate.
