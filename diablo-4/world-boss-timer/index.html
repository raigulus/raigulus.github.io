Diablo 4 World Boss Timer: Next Spawn Today & Live Countdown

World Bosses in Diablo 4 spawn every 3.5 hours (210 minutes) on a fixed global cycle and stay for a 15-minute kill window. The cycle does not reset at midnight: each spawn lands 30 minutes later than the same slot the day before, so the times shift through the week. How the 3.5-Hour Cycle Shifts

Because 3.5 hours does not divide evenly into 24 hours, there is no single daily list of spawn times. Each cycle moves the clock forward 30 minutes, so a spawn at 00:00 UTC on 30 October is followed by 00:30 UTC on 31 October, 01:00 UTC on 1 November, and so on. Which World Boss Spawns Next?

We do not publish an answer, and that is a deliberate choice rather than a gap.

Blizzard has never documented the World Boss rotation. Community trackers reverse-engineer it by logging real spawns, and the pattern they find changes between seasons - earlier seasons ran in a three-two-three-two sequence, and seasonal seasons add their own boss to the pool. Several trackers get it right by tracking what actually spawned, not by predicting it.

What is documented, and therefore what this page gives you, is the timing: a 3.5-hour cycle with a 15-minute window. If a tracker claims to know the boss before it appears, treat it as a guess until the map icon confirms it.

Tips for World Boss Kills

Tip 1

Watch the Map, Not the Timer

The boss icon appears on your map shortly before the spawn. Once it is up, teleport in - the timer only tells you when the window opens, not where it lands.

Tip 2

Group Up

World Bosses are server-wide events, so other players are already fighting what you are farming. Stack up and the 15-minute window is rarely the problem.

Tip 3

Come Back for the Cache

The guaranteed reward cache only drops once per day. If you miss the window, the next spawn is another three and a half hours away.

FAQ

When is the next Diablo 4 World Boss?
World Bosses spawn on a 3.5-hour cycle, which is 210 minutes. The table above lists the next 12 spawn times in UTC and in your own time zone, projected from the current cycle anchor.
How long do I have to kill a World Boss?
You have 15 minutes from spawn before the boss despawns. Blizzard announces the spawn 30 minutes ahead, and the boss icon appears on your map so you can teleport to it.
Which World Boss spawns next?
This page does not publish a guess. Blizzard has never documented the World Boss rotation, and the order changes between seasons as seasonal bosses join it. The spawn times here are documented; the boss order is not.
What does a Diablo 4 World Boss drop?
Legendary items and summoning materials, plus experience for nearby players and Gilding Crests for masterworking gear. World Bosses also drop a guaranteed reward cache once per day, and each boss has its own cosmetic rewards.
Can I get an alert before a World Boss spawns?
Yes. Pick how far ahead you want to be warned on this page and it will send a browser notification and play a short tone. The choice is stored in your own browser only.
Where does a World Boss spawn?
World Bosses appear at fixed gathering points in the open world. The boss icon shows on your map shortly before the spawn, and you can teleport straight to it.
