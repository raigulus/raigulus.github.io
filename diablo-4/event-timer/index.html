World events in Diablo 4 follow fixed schedules. Check this timer regularly to never miss a Helltide or World Boss spawn.

FAQ

How often do Helltides spawn in Diablo 4?
Helltides spawn every hour on the hour and last for 55 minutes. After one ends, the next one begins after a 5-minute gap.
What time do World Bosses spawn in Diablo 4?
World Bosses spawn every 3.5 hours and remain in the world for 15 minutes. They are deterministic and calculable from a fixed epoch.
How often are Legion Events in Diablo 4?
Legion Events spawn every 25 minutes and last for approximately 4 minutes. They are the most frequent world event in Diablo 4.
Are Diablo 4 events server-wide or local?
Helltides and World Bosses are server-wide events visible to all players. Legion Events are zone-specific and appear in random locations.
