Diablo 4 / Helltide Schedule

Diablo 4 Helltide Schedule & Chest Farming

Unofficial fan guide. Helltides are the backbone farm of Diablo 4: they spawn every hour on the hour, last 55 minutes, and drop Aberrant Cinders for targeted chests. For the live countdown, check the Diablo 4 event timer.

Helltide Schedule

SpawnEvery hour on the hour
Duration55 minutes per Helltide
Gap5 minutes between Helltides
ScopeServer-wide event, visible to all players

How the Hourly Cycle Works

Each hourly Helltide lasts 55 minutes, which leaves a 5-minute gap before the next one starts on the hour. The schedule is deterministic, so spawn times never drift. Use the gap to salvage loot, repair gear, and travel to the next Helltide zone before it opens.

Helltide Chests

Aberrant CindersDrop from killed monsters during Helltide
Tortured GiftsSpend cinders for targeted gear and materials
Living SteelFrom Tortured Gifts of Living Steel chests
Murmuring ObolsFrom Tortured Gifts of Mysteries chests

Tips for Helltide Farming

Tip 1

Farm Cinders First

Clear dense monster packs early in the hour to bank Aberrant Cinders, then spend them at Tortured Gift chests before the Helltide ends. Unspent cinders disappear with the event.

Tip 2

Target Living Steel

Tortured Gifts of Living Steel chests are the priority when they appear, since Living Steel feeds endgame boss summoning. Plan your route around them.

Tip 3

Watch the Clock

Chests cost a fixed amount of cinders, so start spending with at least 10 minutes left. The live event timer shows exactly how much time remains.

FAQ

How often do Helltides spawn in Diablo 4?
Helltides spawn every hour on the hour and last for 55 minutes. After one ends, the next one begins after a 5-minute gap.
What are Aberrant Cinders for in Diablo 4?
Aberrant Cinders drop from monsters killed during a Helltide. Spend them at Tortured Gift chests for targeted gear, Living Steel, and Murmuring Obols.
What is the 5-minute Helltide gap?
Each hourly Helltide lasts 55 minutes, leaving a 5-minute gap before the next one starts on the hour. Use the gap to salvage, repair, and travel to the next zone.
Where is the live Helltide timer?
The live Helltide countdown with the next spawn time is on the Diablo 4 event timer.
