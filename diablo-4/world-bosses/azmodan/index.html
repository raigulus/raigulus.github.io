Diablo 4 / World Boss

Azmodan

Azmodan is the Lord of Sin, a demon lord who commands legions from the Burning Hells, and one of the four core Diablo 4 World Bosses.

Azmodan joined the World Boss pool after the original three, which is why older guides still list only Ashava, Avarice and Wandering Death. If a tracker or an old article tells you there are three World Bosses, it is out of date.

Azmodan Rewards

Community trackers do not agree on a named cosmetic set for Azmodan, so this page does not list one. Treat the mount and trophy as a reason to clear the boss rather than as a farm target until a tracker you trust confirms the names.

Legendary itemsEvery World Boss kill drops legendaries.
Summoning materialsMaterials used for endgame boss summons.
Reward cacheOne guaranteed cache per day. Further kills the same day give regular floor loot.
Ancestral itemsJoin the reward pool in Torment III and above.
ExperienceLarge XP for players standing at the spawn.
Gilding CrestsUsed for masterworking gear.

When Azmodan Spawns

World Bosses spawn on a 3.5-hour cycle, which is 210 minutes, and stay in the world for 15 minutes. Blizzard broadcasts a message to all players when the boss is 15 minutes away, and the boss icon appears on your map so you can teleport straight to the spawn point.

The Diablo 4 World Boss timer lists the next twelve spawn times in UTC and in your own time zone, and can send you a browser alert before the window opens. It deliberately does not name the boss for each spawn, because Blizzard does not publish the rotation.

FAQ

When does Azmodan spawn?
World Bosses spawn on a 3.5-hour cycle, and Azmodan appears somewhere in that rotation. Blizzard does not publish the rotation order, so this page does not guess which spawn is next - the [World Boss timer](/diablo-4/world-boss-timer/) lists the next twelve spawn times.
What does Azmodan drop?
Legendary items, summoning materials, experience and Gilding Crests, plus a guaranteed reward cache once per day. In Torment III and above the reward pool includes Ancestral items.
How long do I have to kill it?
Fifteen minutes from the moment it spawns. Blizzard broadcasts a message to all players 15 minutes ahead, and the boss icon appears on your map so you can teleport straight to it.
